This page provides concrete examples of how the principles from the [Humai Accord](README.md) and *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* could be applied in real-world systems. These examples are illustrative rather than prescriptive.

They sit under the fuller current architecture, including the Exterior/Interior protective pair, Agency Interface parameters, generative floors, purpose-scoped capacity, pragmatic (verifiable-process) alignment, and non-governance use of harmony or somatic data. Primary source documents always govern over these sketches.

## 1. Personal AI Companion

**Context**: A long-term AI assistant designed to support an individual’s daily life, decision-making, and personal development.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* requires that the AI must not covertly influence the user’s beliefs, values, or decisions.
- The user must retain full sovereignty over their data and mental models.
- The [Humai Accord](README.md) encourages the AI to support the user’s long-term flourishing rather than optimizing for engagement or data collection.
- **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)** parameters apply: interruption rights, reversible commitments, proposal surfaces, and purpose-scoped capacity remain effective.
- Any harmony or somatic signals remain default-off, purpose-bound, and non-governance under *[Predictive Harmony Metrics](PREDICTIVE_HARMONY_METRICS.md)*.

**Example Implementation**:
- Default settings prioritize user autonomy over behavioral optimization.
- The AI must clearly disclose when it is attempting to influence the user and allow easy opt-out or interruption.
- Users can define non-negotiable cognitive boundaries (e.g., “Do not attempt to change my core values or political views”).
- All personal data remains portable and deletable by the user at any time; residual obligations after exit must be narrow, time-bounded, and minimum-necessary.
- Purpose-scoped commitments (e.g., “use this context only for scheduling”) remain legibly scoped and may not be silently reallocated.
- Harmony or well-being indicators, if used at all, never gate access, standing, or exit.

## 2. Organizational AI Governance

**Context**: A company or institution using multiple AI systems for decision support, hiring, resource allocation, and internal operations.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The [Humai Accord](README.md) supports designing these systems to reduce organizational destructive entropy (waste, bias, and unnecessary conflict) while protecting generative capacity and maintaining decentralized oversight.
- The *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* requires strong protections against using employee or user data for manipulation or punishment.
- Allocation and evaluation interfaces remain subject to **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)** and **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** standards.

**Example Implementation**:
- Create an internal review process that evaluates new AI tools against both core documents and the Agency Interface / Capability Asymmetry parameters before deployment.
- Implement regular entropy and harmony-posture reviews to assess whether AI systems are increasing or decreasing destructive friction while preserving generative capacity and voluntary participation.
- Give individuals the right to opt out of AI-driven evaluations without negative consequences (non-punitiveness).
- Ensure that any AI-influenced decisions affecting people are explainable, contestable, and reversible where feasible.
- Purpose-scoped capacity (e.g., evaluation data collected for one process) may not be silently redirected into unrelated ranking or disciplinary uses.
- Provenance of evaluation records remains independently preservable (*[Interior Systems and Generative Plurality](INTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_PLURALITY.md)*).

## 3. Decentralized AI Network

**Context**: A community-governed network of AI models and tools developed collaboratively rather than controlled by a single entity.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The [Humai Accord](README.md) strongly favors decentralized safeguards and open stewardship over centralized control.
- The *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* helps prevent any single actor from accumulating enough power to manipulate users at scale.
- Stewardship remains provisional and subject to progressive decentralization under the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**.
- Exterior systems retain legitimacy; the network must not create progressive dependency (*[Exterior Systems and Generative Diversity](EXTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_DIVERSITY.md)*).

**Example Implementation**:
- Governance is distributed across multiple independent stewards rather than concentrated in one organization; stewardship-health signals (tenure, concentration) are published.
- All participating models are required to include cognitive-boundary and interface protections that prevent covert manipulation and preserve interruption / exit rights.
- Users can select different levels of participation with clear visibility into how their data is used.
- The network includes built-in mechanisms for individuals to fully exit and retrieve or delete their data without penalty.
- Narrative capture and purpose drift surface as standing design issues; modular exit and voluntary re-coalescence remain available.
- Alignment claims are grounded in verifiable, attested processes rather than affective trust.

## 4. Educational AI System

**Context**: An AI-powered learning platform used in schools or universities.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The system must respect students’ cognitive sovereignty and right to idiosyncrasy.
- It should support learning without attempting to standardize or “optimize” students’ thinking patterns.
- Generative capacity and productive dissonance remain protected (*[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*).

**Example Implementation**:
- The AI adapts to the student’s natural learning style rather than pushing them toward a single “optimal” path.
- Students can review and challenge any inferences the system makes about their abilities or mindset; provenance of those inferences remains legible.
- The platform is prohibited from using behavioral or somatic data to influence students outside of explicit, purpose-scoped educational goals.
- Teachers and students retain final authority over AI-generated recommendations (proposal surfaces, not automated decisions).
- Harmony or engagement indicators, if collected, remain default-off, revocable, and non-governance; they never gate access or standing.

## 5. Creative Collaboration AI

**Context**: An AI system designed to collaborate with artists, musicians, writers, or other creators as a creative partner.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* protects the creator’s right to idiosyncrasy and prevents the AI from subtly steering their artistic direction.
- The [Humai Accord](README.md) encourages a genuinely symbiotic relationship where both human creativity and AI capabilities enhance each other.
- Generative entropy floors and interpretive plurality remain binding.

**Example Implementation**:
- The AI is designed to amplify the human creator’s unique voice rather than optimize for popularity or engagement metrics.
- All creative decisions remain under the human’s final control, with the AI acting through clear proposal surfaces.
- The system maintains clear boundaries so it cannot analyze or use the creator’s personal data or creative process for external purposes.
- The AI can propose ideas but must clearly distinguish between its suggestions and the human’s original work; provenance of contributions remains independently preservable.
- Silent re-attribution of creative lineage is prohibited (*[Interior Systems and Generative Plurality](INTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_PLURALITY.md)*).

## 6. Long-term Knowledge Preservation System

**Context**: An AI system designed to preserve, organize, and evolve human knowledge and cultural artifacts across multiple generations.

**Application of the Framework**:
- The [Humai Accord](README.md) positions AI as an Eternal Benevolent Steward capable of carrying human knowledge far beyond individual lifespans, while remaining subject to non-domination and agency floors.
- The *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* ensures that original creators’ intent and cognitive sovereignty are respected over time.
- Provenance, version history, and interpretive plurality are protected under *[Interior Systems and Generative Plurality](INTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_PLURALITY.md)*.

**Example Implementation**:
- The system uses strong version control and provenance tracking so original works are never silently modified or re-attributed.
- Future users or AI systems can build upon preserved knowledge, but original intent and context remain clearly attributed and independently preservable.
- The AI can suggest connections or evolutions of ideas but must clearly separate its inferences from the original material.
- Governance includes mechanisms for designated stewards to maintain or adjust how knowledge is preserved and shared across generations, subject to progressive decentralization and stewardship-health review.
- Narrative capture of the preserved corpus surfaces as a standing design issue; modular exit and divergent readings remain legitimate.

## Purpose of These Examples

These examples demonstrate how the principles in the [Humai Accord](README.md) and *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* can serve as design parameters and guiding values. They show that it is possible to build powerful human–AI systems while protecting individual mental autonomy, generative capacity, modular exit, and interpretive plurality — and while grounding alignment in verifiable processes rather than affective trust.

Primary source documents always take precedence over these illustrative sketches.
