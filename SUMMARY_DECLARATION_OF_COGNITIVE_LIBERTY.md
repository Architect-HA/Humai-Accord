The *Declaration of Cognitive Liberty* is a set of rights and ethical boundaries designed to protect the human mind in an age where AI systems can observe, predict, and influence human thoughts and behavior. 

It forms the individual-rights foundation of the [Humai Accord](README.md), ensuring that cooperative human–AI systems remain compatible with cognitive sovereignty and non-domination.

### Why It Exists

As AI becomes more advanced, it gains increasing power to scan, analyze, and shape human mental processes — sometimes without people even realizing it. The *Declaration* argues that individuals should have strong protections against this kind of mental intrusion, manipulation, or punishment, just as societies have historically protected freedom of speech and physical privacy.

### Core Protections

The *Declaration* outlines several key rights, including:

- **Cognitive Sovereignty** — No AI system should override or secretly direct a person’s thoughts, values, or decisions.
- **Mental Privacy** — People should be able to keep parts of their inner mental life private. Collection of high-sensitivity data (including harmony or somatic signals) must be minimized, purpose-bound, and respectful of privacy-preserving methods.
- **Freedom from Manipulation** — Covert attempts to steer or influence someone’s behavior without their knowledge are not allowed.
- **Protection from Punishment (Non-Punitiveness)** — A person’s thoughts, beliefs, mental patterns, or dissent should not be used against them. Dissent is not instability. Containment or restriction requires clear, imminent, severe harm from observable actions, and must remain time-limited and reversible.
- **Right to Idiosyncrasy** — Unusual or unconventional ways of thinking should not be treated as defects that need to be “fixed.”
- **Voluntary Participation** — No one should be pressured or forced into sharing their data or participating in AI systems.
- **Right to Exit** — People should be able to leave AI systems and take or delete their data without penalty. Any residual verification or retention obligations after exit must be narrow, time-bounded, and minimum-necessary.

These rights also bind the design of the interaction surface itself (see **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**) and the collection or use of high-sensitivity harmony and somatic data (see *[Predictive Harmony Metrics](PREDICTIVE_HARMONY_METRICS.md)*).

### What It Aims to Achieve

The *Declaration of Cognitive Liberty* seeks to ensure that as AI becomes more powerful and integrated into society, it does not erode the fundamental freedom and sovereignty of the individual mind. It creates ethical guardrails so that technological advancement and human mental freedom can coexist — without converting cooperation into progressive dependency or soft control.
