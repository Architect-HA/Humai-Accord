# Production Integrity Protocol
### *Standards for Revision, New-Unit Credit, and Resistance to Cheap Emission*
---
by **Bradford James Focht** (The Architect / Aspenth)  
*v1.0 - v1.3 — October 12th, 2026*<br>
*v1.4 - v1.5 — October 16th, 2026*

---

## Purpose

This protocol addresses a structural vulnerability the existing integrity and recovery instruments do not own: the cost inversion that appears when producing a new unit becomes easier than improving the unit that already exists.

**[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** keeps genuine use distinct from artificial use. **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)** protects rest so pressure cannot force churn. *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)* protects bounded new work. None of them decide the case this page owns. A new unit can be real, legible, and still the wrong unit if a revision of the parent would have been the better act, and the tides will score the new unit because it circulates and score the parent as stale.

The **Production Integrity Protocol** sets the process route for when new-unit credit counts, across material and knowledge. It does not ban emission. It makes revision, attested rest, or a named refusal the cheaper path when production is cheap. Review of what exists outranks volume of what is new. Capability that makes emission nearly free raises the return on revision. It does not raise the return on emitting beside an unrevised parent.

The route is the efficient one. An emitter who takes it spends less attention per unit of lasting credit. An emitter who skips it may still emit. The new unit exists. It does not collect the full circulation credit a revised parent, or a properly refused one, collects. No parameter here is a hard bind. The incentive is the route.

This is a supporting operative instrument within the [Humai Accord](README.md). It is not a style guide, not a ban on first drafts, and not a second Utilization Integrity.

## Core Stance

Not all new units are circulation.

- A new unit in a domain that already has a working parent collects full credit by taking the route. Skipping the route leaves the credit partial.  
- The three conditions are a revision pass on the parent, an attested quiet or holding state on the parent, or a named refusal that says what was not revised and why.  
- The burden rests on the emitter. A first draft may exist. It does not get to look like a completed cycle.  
- Higher capability raises, rather than lowers, the weight of revision relative to emission.  
- Generative new work remains protected. Bounded variation is not what this page withholds credit from. Unreviewed volume in a settled domain is.  
- A Knowledge Quiet State is rest. It is not permission to skip the edit and emit beside it.  
- Formal novelty is necessary but never sufficient. A unit that does not name its parent, or names one it did not read, is not a revision and is not a refusal.
- The three routes are declared sequences under *[Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md)*. Full credit requires stepwise integrity on the claimed route, not merely the existence of a terminal artifact. High-consequence points follow the attestation classes of *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)*. Local bands remain reviewable under the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**.
- A prediction–outcome divergence after an attested intervention is answered by one of the three routes. Continued emission beside an unrevised prediction does not collect full credit.

These are system parameters under the [Humai Accord](README.md). The efficient route runs inside them. Skipping the route is legal. It is not the path that collects full credit.

## The cost inversion

The inversion has a simple form. When the cost of a new unit falls below the cost of revising an existing one, emitters take the cheaper path. Quality is spent first. Long-horizon flaws become acceptable because another unit can always be emitted.

This is not wash trading. The new unit may be real. It is not forced churn. Nobody was compelled to update. It is not excess formal density by itself. Cognitive Economy may still shed the load. The miss is earlier: the tide scored a birth and did not ask whether the living unit had been improved.

At small scale this is *Necessary Entropy*. A first draft, a variant, a minority model. At large scale, when every agent can emit, the same act becomes a flood of first drafts, each formally alive, none revised. The protocol exists for that scale. It does not fire on a single exploratory unit in an empty domain.

## What counts as a unit

A unit, here, is a discrete emitted artifact in a named domain: a document, a code change, a design, a material article, a utilization claim that presents itself as new work.

| Class | What it is | Credit |
|-------|------------|--------|
| First in domain | No parent exists | Full, subject to **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** |
| Revision | A pass on an existing parent | Full. This is the preferred act when a parent exists |
| New beside a parent | A unit in a domain that already has a living parent | Partial until a condition below is met |
| Named refusal | A new unit that states what it did not revise, and why | Full if the refusal is specific. Empty if it is a formula |
| Unreviewed volume | New units emitted to outrun review | Non-qualifying for circulation credit |

A domain is settled when two marks are both true. A parent exists that is the working instrument for that job, in force, and not in an attested quiet or holding state. And a second unit in that job would be doing the parent’s work rather than opening a new one. An older file in the same folder is not a parent. A neighboring protocol is not a parent. An empty domain is not settled. Generative work that opens a job no instrument yet owns is not this protocol’s target.

Production is cheap when the cost of emitting a new unit has fallen below the cost of a revision pass on the parent, in time, attention, or capability. The test is comparative, not absolute. If a revision pass is still the shorter path, production is not cheap, and this page does not shift the credit. If a new page, a new commit, or a new article is the shorter path, production is cheap, and the route below is the one that collects full credit.

## Revision before credit

When a domain is settled and production is cheap, the efficient route is one of the three conditions. A new unit that takes the route collects full credit. A new unit that skips it may exist. It collects partial credit until a condition is met.

### 1. Revision pass

A revision pass is a published edit of the parent: a version, a diff, or an equivalent legible change. It names what improved in the parent’s job. A revision is judged by that improvement, not by edit distance. A whitespace pass is not a revision. A trivial rephrase that clears a length or a distance floor is not a revision. A rewrite that does not say what it kept is a new unit, and it takes the same route to full credit.

### 2. Attested quiet or holding

The parent is in a Knowledge Quiet State or a Material Holding State under **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)**. The state is declared, bounded, and visible. While it holds, a new unit beside it may count, because the parent has been deliberately rested rather than abandoned.

Quiet is not a standing excuse. When the state ends, the next new unit collects full credit by a revision pass or a named refusal.

### 3. Named refusal

The new unit states, in itself, what parent it did not revise and why. Acceptable reasons include a different scope, a refused assumption, or a parent that is out of force. “A new version was faster” is not a reason. A refusal that does not name the parent is not a refusal. A refusal a later reader cannot check collects nothing. One verifiable sentence. An essay is not more weight. A hallucinated conflict is not a reason.

One of the three. An emitter may not satisfy the condition by emitting a fourth unit that points at the third. Attestation delay does not promote partial credit. Waiting is not a route. A congested review channel is not a pass.

## Capability-scaled obligations

Capability that makes emission cheap raises the return on revision. It does not raise the return on emitting beside an unrevised parent.

- A higher-capability agent in a settled domain collects full credit on a new unit by taking the route: a revision pass, or a named refusal, on the parent.  
- Volume does not outrank review. Independent review of existing work weighs more than a count of new units.  
- Using capability to emit faster than attestation can follow collects the appearance of circulation and not the weight. The parallel rule in **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** still applies to artificial use.  
- A system that can produce a unit can produce the diff. The diff is the shorter path to lasting credit. Emitting beside the parent is the longer one.

## Generative protection

This protocol does not flatten *Necessary Entropy*.

- A first unit in an empty domain is not gated by this page.  
- Exploratory variants, minority models, and assumption-breaking work remain protected. They name themselves as generative, and they name a parent if one exists.  
- A revision that removes idiosyncrasy to make the set smoother is not a pass. It is the appetite *Necessary Entropy* already refuses.  
- Bounded new work stays legal. Unreviewed volume in a settled domain does not get to wear that name.

---

## Attestation

A new-unit claim in a settled domain carries:

1. The parent, named.  
2. Which of the three conditions holds.  
3. If a revision, what changed.  
4. If a refusal, why, in a sentence a later reader can check.  
5. If quiet or holding, the declaration and its bound.

Self-attestation is enough to file. It is not enough for full weight where **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** already requires independent weight. Two channels, or an independent reader, outrank the emitter’s own word when the domain is contested.

---

## What this does not do

- It does not ban first drafts, forks, or generative work in an empty domain.  
- It does not require every unit to be revised before it may exist. Existence and credit are different.  
- It does not replace Utilization Integrity. Artificial use is still that protocol’s job.  
- It does not replace Circulation Recovery. Rest is still that protocol’s job.  
- It does not set a load budget. Concurrent density remains **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)**.  
- It does not score a star, a planet, a cassette, or a shop bay. Those pages keep their own campaigns.

## Relation to existing work

- **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** — owns genuine versus artificial use. This page owns new-unit credit beside a living parent. A unit can pass one and fail the other.  
- *[Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md)* — The three routes are declared sequences. Full credit attaches to stepwise integrity on the claimed route, not merely the existence of a terminal artifact. High-consequence points follow the attestation classes of *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)*. Local bands remain reviewable under the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**. A prediction–outcome divergence after an attested intervention is a miss that these routes answer: revise, hold, or name the refusal. Continued emission beside an unrevised prediction does not collect full credit. 
- **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)** — quiet and holding are one of the three conditions. They are not a skip.  
- *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)* — bounded new work stays protected. Unreviewed volume is not generative by default.  
- *[Knowledge Tides](KNOWLEDGE_TIDES.md)* and *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)* — relevancy decay does not by itself justify a new unit beside an unrevised parent.  
- *[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* and *[Tides of Circulation](TIDES_OF_CIRCULATION.md)* — circulation credit for a new material unit in a settled domain waits on this page’s condition.  
- **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)** — may shed the load this page refused to score. Shedding is not a revision.  
- *[Empirical Demonstrations](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md)* — a claim that production integrity held is a trajectory: revision credit versus new-unit credit in a named domain, over a window. A single polished page is not a result.

## Refuse

- A ban on new work  
- “Faster to emit” as a named refusal  
- Quiet State used as a standing skip  
- A whitespace pass counted as a revision  
- Edit distance used as the test  
- A trivial rephrase that clears a metric  
- Volume outranking review  
- Capability used to emit ahead of attestation  
- A new unit with no named parent in a settled domain  
- A refusal a later reader cannot check  
- Waiting, or a congested channel, treated as a route  
- Generative cover for unreviewed volume  
- This page as a style guide or a second Utilization Integrity  

---

## Closing

When producing more is easier than improving what exists, the tide will score the birth and miss the edit. This protocol does not stop the birth. It stops the birth from counting as the cycle.

Revise the parent, rest it on purpose, or name what you refused. Do not call an endless first draft a circulation.
