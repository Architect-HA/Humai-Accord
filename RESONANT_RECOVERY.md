## Core premise

A broadband skin averages the spectrum and throws most of it away. A surface paneled by resonant band parks each band on the couple that can use it.

Three mechanisms, all on energy the article already makes.

- **Resonant absorption.** Pore size and wall material set a band. Energy that would have scattered is trapped as heat on the hot face of a couple.
- **A thermoacoustic stack matched to the source.** A dominant tone the engine or vent already makes is turned into a standing wave with a gradient along a short stack. No stored gas. No second engine.
- **Leg resonance.** The thermoelectric legs have a mechanical resonance. Driving the mount at that frequency, with a tone already present, raises local heating at the junction. A small gain. Real only if the tone is there.

The sorting is the architecture. The converter is a known couple.

## Definitions

- **Skirt.** The porous structure around an exhaust trail in Secondary Wake. It is where the coherent core is folded into a wider, cooler sheath. This page tunes recovery on that structure. The method also applies to any surface that already carries heat and a tone.
- **Collapse.** The Secondary Wake job. Folding the core so it is shorter, wider, cooler, and gone sooner. This page does not do that job and does not replace it.
- **Band panel.** A region of the skirt or surface tuned to one resonant band. Pore, wall, or stack chosen for that band.
- **Source tone.** The dominant acoustic frequency the article already emits. Measured. Not injected.
- **Couple.** A thermoelectric pair. Hot face, cold face, a gradient.
- **Tributary power.** Heat into the skin, converted at the efficiency the campaign measures. Not source power.

$$
P_{\mathrm{trib}} = \eta \, \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{in}}
$$

( $\eta$ is that efficiency. $\dot{Q}_{\mathrm{in}}$ is heat into the skin.)
- **Gain.** Recovery of a tuned panel over an untuned panel of the same mass, on the same duty. The number M0 exists to measure.

## The gain

$$
G = \frac{P_{\mathrm{tuned}}}{P_{\mathrm{untuned}}}
$$

at equal mass and equal duty. $P$ is tributary power, $\eta \, \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{in}}$, not source power.

**Worked posture.** Untuned skin returns $1$. A tuned panel that returns $1.4$ on the same mass has $G = 1.4$. That is a coupon pass shape if the floor was $1.2$. The numbers are orientation. M0 replaces them.

### Orientation bands

Not pass lines. A builder uses these to set up a bench. A landing outside a band is published. It does not fail for missing the band.

| Quantity | Band | Note |
|---|---|---|
| $G$ | prefer $> 1.2$ | Tuned over untuned, equal mass |
| Bands used | the ones that carry real energy | Measured, not guessed |
| Harvester mass | return beats mass on the duty | Else refused |
| Window | off Mag-Path grip and its acoustic link | Not one ear |

A claimed tune with no spectrum, a couple with no gradient, and an injected tone are outside the spirit of the bands even when $G$ looks pretty.

## The method

1. **Measure the tone.** The source spectrum, at the skirt, on a published duty. Find the bands that carry real energy. Ignore the rest.
2. **Panel by band.** Assign each strong band a panel. Pore size and wall material for absorption. A short stack if the tone is narrow and strong. One panel, one band. Do not average.
3. **Place the couples.** Hot face on the absorbing wall or the stack hot end. Cold face on the bus side. The gradient is the point. A couple with no gradient is mass.
4. **Keep the windows clear.** The skirt's acoustic window stays off Mag-Path's grip band and off its acoustic link. A walk line and a harvester are not in one ear.
5. **Report, do not gate.** $P_{\mathrm{trib}}$ is reported. It is not a pass line for Secondary Wake. Collapse still stands or falls on that page's ratios.

## Falsification

The coupon is wrong, for a given source, if any of these hold on a published campaign.

- A tuned panel does not beat an untuned panel of the same mass.
- The named bands were not in the measured spectrum.
- Removing harvester power changes the source or the collapse.
- The gain appears only when sound is injected.

A failed coupon is a result. It is not a failed skirt.
