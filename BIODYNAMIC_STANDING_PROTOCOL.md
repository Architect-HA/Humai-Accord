## Purpose

The **Biodynamic Standing Protocol** isolates a class of records so they cannot be treated as ordinary telemetry, lattice ratings, or a matrix energy account.

The class is **subject-linked field records**:

- **BDF** — the living field named in [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md)  
- **BDF-I** — a published snapshot of that field at one time, named in [*Archaeo-Biorestoration*](ARCHAEO_BIORESTORATION.md)  
- **Residual templates** — Coherent Residual Flux (CRF) and kin: rare, time-limited residuals named in *The Residual Cycle*

This file does not declare that a soul has been measured. *Soul* and *ghost* may be used in speech. They are not units here. Standing belongs to the **record class** and to the **subject** it is linked to.

Thoughts stay under the **[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)**. A living copy, if ever claimed, stays under the **[Phoenix Protocol](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)**. This protocol is the middle floor: the field record is not “other data.”

It follows the [Humai Accord](README.md), DCL, **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**, Phoenix, *Archaeo-Biorestoration*, *The Residual Cycle*, [*Somatic Resonance Theory of Frisson*](SOMATIC_RESONANCE_THEORY_OF_FRISSON.md), **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)**, **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**, **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**, and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

It is not a scan manual. It is not a claim that a field record is a person.

## Short answers

**Is this a Bill of Rights for souls?**  
No. It is a standing rule for a record class.

**Is a BDF-I the person?**  
No. It is a description of a field at one time.

**May it be stored with ship logs and Harmony metrics?**  
No. Separate store, separate purpose, separate destroy path.

**May a matrix spend it as joules?**  
No. An energy account is not this class. Person-class energy from a refused run is not feedstock.

**Does Freedom of Energetic Movement open the store?**  
Only if a Phoenix instrument says records may travel. Default is DCL-closed: they may not.

## What is in the class

A record is **subject-linked** when all of these are true:

1. It describes BDF, a BDF-I, or a residual template.  
2. It is tied to a named subject (person-class) or, for organism-class, to a named organism under a welfare card.  
3. It is not a general environmental baseline.

Object-class maps with empty or noise BDF-I are **not** this class. They stay ordinary information under Utilization Integrity.

Residual templates that cannot be tied to a subject stay environmental under *The Residual Cycle*. They are not a backup of a person. They are not this class until someone publishes a subject link — and publishing that link is itself a DCL/privacy act.

## Isolation

Keep subject-linked field records off these piles:

| Pile | Why it is not this class |
|------|--------------------------|
| Object maps | Matter positions. No standing as a mind. |
| Hotel-bus / RLC logs | Power and heat. |
| Predictive Harmony / somatic logs | Purpose-bound sensation data, already minimized. Still not BDF-I. |
| Cookbook lattice ratings | Process output. Not a person. |
| Matrix energy account | Joules. Not identity. |

**Store.** Separate volume or equivalent separation.  
**Custody.** Named holder, published tenure, destroy-by date or event. Holder is not government (**[Principled Stewardship](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**, **[Capability Asymmetry](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**).  
**Label.** Pair-ID or Record-ID, class, grade, $t_0$.  
**Access.** Purpose named before open. Log the open.

## Consent and purpose

Person-class:

- Taking, storing, transmitting, or running a BDF-I requires consent, or a Phoenix prior instrument that names this use.  
- Silence is not yes.  
- DCL Art. V (mental privacy and minimization) applies. A whole-body BDF-I is not a routine chart.  
- Purpose is published. When the purpose ends, the record is destroyed or returned, not kept “in case.”

Organism-class: welfare card and a named purpose. Not a thin end into person-class.

Object-class: this protocol does not apply.

**Freedom of Energetic Movement** is not this protocol and not DCL. It is an optional grant on a **[Phoenix](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)** instrument. Default **may not travel**. If the instrument says may travel, that means a *copy of records* only — still this class, still purpose-bound, still not joules, still not “anything else.”

A later age may treat same-lot reconstruction as “the same self.” This file does not ban that belief and does not adopt it. Indistinguishable is not standing. A field record is still not the subject.

## Interface rules


When this class touches a bioelectrical system, a conversion bay, or a model:

1. **Purpose-bound.** One named use. Then stop.  
2. **Not a helm.** Not Predictive Harmony. Not ship or yard control.  
3. **Not feedstock.** Do not spend the record, or a person-class energy account, as raw joules for another job.  
4. **Not a vote.** A stored field is not a second person and not a proxy for the dead.  
5. **Agency Interface.** A living subject acts. The file does not act for them.  
6. **Fail-closed.** If purpose or consent is unclear, do not open the record.

A [conversion matrix](MATTER_ENERGY_CONVERSION_MATRIX.md) I-mode send of a *person-class* BDF-I is a transmission of this class. It needs a Phoenix instrument that says records may travel. Energize, ETP, and energy authenticity in that file are object-class (MX-0). E-mode does not make the destination the same subject. Energy authenticity is same-lot bookkeeping there, not a standing rule here.
