## Purpose

This document is a general theory for any engine, vent, or process that leaves a coherent energetic trail. The trail may be a rocket plume, a station exhaust, a drill-heat wake, or any signature whose structure persists longer than the job requires.

The aim is not to hide the trail. The aim is to spend its coherence. A coherent core cooks what follows it, sits in a lane, and radiates as a long signature. A collapsed remainder is wider, cooler, and gone sooner. Recovery of low-grade heat is a tributary. It is not the reason to fly the article.

This is an architecture and a campaign. It is not a nozzle stamp and not a stealth kit.

It follows the [Humai Accord](README.md), including [*Necessary Entropy*](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md), [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md), *[Stormcrashers](STORMCRASHERS.md)*, the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Architectural Elasticity Protocol](ARCHITECTURAL_ELASTICITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

## Scope and Limits

**In scope**

- Any directed energetic exhaust whose core stays coherent past the nozzle or the vent
- A short porous skirt and a ring of independent mixers that fold the trail into itself
- Atmosphere and vacuum as two instances of one law
- A thermoelectric tributary on the skirt, optional
- A falsifiable campaign: core length, radiating area, peak temperature, engine independence

**Out of scope**

- A longer nozzle as the solution
- Injected salt, helium, or water as the first article
- A claim that the trail is invisible
- A weaponized jet, a directed exhaust, or a stealth purpose
- A skirt the engine needs in order to run

A failed mixer isolates. The source still runs.

## Short answers

**Does this hide a plume?**  
No. It spends the plume’s coherence. A cooler, wider remainder is a consequence. Invisibility is not a pass.

**Is recovery required?**  
No. A thermoelectric skin may feed a bus. It does not size the article. A skirt that recovers and does not collapse has failed.

**Atmosphere or vacuum?**  
Both are instances of one law. A pass in one does not cover a fail in the other.

**What if a mixer clogs?**  
That mixer is a remainder. The source still runs. The ring still collapses, or the minimum live count is revised in public.

**What if the source needs the skirt?**  
The article has become a nozzle. That is a failed pass. Later credit is zero.

**Does the atmosphere power the skirt?**  
No. The layer is the sink and the witness. It is not a fuel.

## Core premise

A trail is a residue. [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md) already treats a quiet medium as a layer that holds what passed through it. A wake is that layer, hot and structured, for a while.

[*Necessary Entropy*](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md) prefers the cascade. Do not preserve the core. Help it fall to smaller scales until it is a wide, cool, fast-radiating remainder.

[*Stormcrashers*](STORMCRASHERS.md) dissipates a coherent wind by porosity, not by a wall. Shelter there is an array property. Collapse here is an array property. One mixer is not a switch.

The secondary wake is the deliberate weaker, wider sheath the primary trail is folded into. The primary does not stream. It collapses into the sheath, and the sheath radiates.

## Definitions

**Trail.** The coherent energetic exhaust downstream of a source. A plume, a vent wake, a heat lane.

**Core.** The region of the trail whose temperature and structure remain above a published threshold. Length $L$. Peak temperature $T_c$.

**Secondary wake.** The wider, cooler sheath produced by folding the core into itself through a porous skirt and a mixer ring. Not a second engine.

**Remainder.** What is left after collapse: larger radiating area, lower peak, shorter coherence. A remainder is a result. It is not a clean sky.

**Mixer.** One independent element of the ring. A clog is a remainder, in the same spirit as a [Gulpgates](GULPGATES.md) array. It is not a lost source.

**Tributary.** Optional thermoelectric recovery on the skirt. It may feed a bus. It does not size the article. A cold system does not depend on the source burning. Same posture as [Mag-Path](MAG_PATH.md) heat and the [Regenerative Lattice Core](REGENERATIVE_LATTICE_CORE.md) reject bus.

**Signature proxy.** A published band intensity or a radiative proxy. Not a detection claim.

## The Law

Collapse is a change in three numbers, measured on the same source with the skirt off and on.

$$
\Lambda = \frac{L_{\mathrm{on}}}{L_{\mathrm{off}}}, \quad
\alpha = \frac{A_{\mathrm{on}}}{A_{\mathrm{off}}}, \quad
\theta = \frac{T_{c,\mathrm{on}}}{T_{c,\mathrm{off}}}
$$

$L$ is core length. $A$ is the area above a published temperature or radiance floor. $T_c$ is peak core temperature.

A pass requires

$$
\Lambda < 1, \quad \alpha > 1, \quad \theta < 1, \quad \tau_{\mathrm{on}} < \tau_{\mathrm{off}}
$$

and the source runs with the skirt failed open. If the source needs the skirt, the article has become a nozzle. That is a failed pass. Under [Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md), a source that will not run without the skirt zeros the collapse credit. The endpoint does not wash a skipped independence check.

$\tau$ is the time for the core to fall below the published threshold after the source stops, or the time to reach $\Lambda$ on a steady burn. A short core that lingers has not collapsed. Time is part of the law.

Radiative proxy, orientation only:

$$
S \propto \varepsilon \, A \, T_c^{4}
$$

$S$ is not the pass line. $\Lambda$, $\alpha$, $\theta$, and $\tau$ are. A lower $S$ is a consequence. It is not a stealth claim. Emissivity $\varepsilon$ is published with the test, or taken as $1$ for the proxy.

**Harm line.** Collapse is also for what follows the trail. At a published standoff $d$, heat flux or radiance on a trailer or a dock face is $q(d)$. A pass prefers $q_{\mathrm{on}}(d) < q_{\mathrm{off}}(d)$. It does not replace $\Lambda$, $\alpha$, $\theta$, and $\tau$. A skirt that shortens the core and cooks the trailer has not finished the job.

**Worked posture.** Skirt off: $L = 40\,\mathrm{m}$, $A = 20\,\mathrm{m}^{2}$, $T_c = 1800\,\mathrm{K}$, $\tau = 12\,\mathrm{s}$. Skirt on: $L = 18\,\mathrm{m}$, $A = 55\,\mathrm{m}^{2}$, $T_c = 1100\,\mathrm{K}$, $\tau = 5\,\mathrm{s}$. Then $\Lambda = 0.45$, $\alpha = 2.75$, $\theta = 0.61$, and $\tau$ fell. That is a pass shape. The numbers are orientation. M0 replaces them.

### Orientation bands

Not pass lines. A builder uses these to set up a bench. A campaign that lands outside a band publishes the landing. It does not fail for missing the band. M0 replaces every figure here.

| Quantity | Band | Why this wide |

| $\Lambda$ | $0.3\text{–}0.7$ | Core clearly shorter, not a rounding error and not a vanished plume |
| $\alpha$ | $1.5\text{–}4$ | Radiating area grown, not a trickle and not an unbounded claim |
| $\theta$ | $0.5\text{–}0.85$ | Peak down, still a hot remainder |
| $\tau_{\mathrm{on}}/\tau_{\mathrm{off}}$ | $0.3\text{–}0.7$ | Dies sooner. Same shape as $\Lambda$ |
| $\phi$ | $0.4\text{–}0.8$ | Porous. Below $0.2$ is nearly a shroud. Above $0.9$ may not fold the trail |
| $n$ | $4\text{–}12$ | A ring. Two is a pair, not an array. More than twelve is a later station |
| $n_{\min}/n$ | $\ge 0.5$ | Half the ring still collapses, or the article is a single point |
| $d$ | $5\text{–}30\,\mathrm{m}$ | A dock lane or a trailer gap. Not a nozzle length. Not a horizon |
| $q_{\mathrm{on}}/q_{\mathrm{off}}$ | $< 1$, prefer $0.4\text{–}0.8$ | Trailer better off. The prefer-band is illustrative |

| Harvester return | beats its mass on the published duty, or the coupon fails | Not a watts target |

A solid shroud, a single mixer, a standoff inside the nozzle, and an $\eta$ cited as the reason to fly are outside the spirit of the bands even when a ratio looks pretty. The falsifiers still govern. These bands do not.

## Mixer ring

$n$ independent mixers on a short skirt. Each sees a share of the trail. None is required.

Porosity of the skirt, orientation:

$$
\phi = 1 - \frac{A_{\mathrm{solid}}}{A_{\mathrm{skirt}}}
$$

$\phi$ is published. A solid shroud is $\phi = 0$ and is refused. A wall does not collapse a trail. It holds one.

A failed mixer drops out. The ring still collapses if

$$
n_{\mathrm{live}} \ge n_{\min}
$$

$n_{\min}$ is a campaign number. One clog is a remainder, not a lost burn.

Injected mass, if any, is a later coupon. The first article folds the trail into itself. It does not spend a stored gas to cool a plume.

## Two instances

The law does not change. The medium does.

**Atmosphere.** The skirt also breaks afterburning structure. Mixing is with the trail and with the air the trail has already entrained. Cabin or habitat air is not the working fluid.

**Vacuum.** There is no air to blow into. Collapse is expansion and radiation. The skirt forces a faster spread and a broader radiating area. $A$ rises. $L$ falls. $T_c$ falls because the same energy occupies more area and leaves faster as radiation.

A campaign that passes in one instance and fails in the other publishes both. It does not average them into a pass.

## The Layer

[*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md) is the sink and the witness. It is not a fuel.

The atmosphere is a reinforcing circulatory layer. Most residual energy disorders quickly into incoherent flux and rejoins mixing, water, and soil. The vacuum is the sparse, long-path case of the same layer. It gathers and dilutes. It does not author a pattern. A quiet volume is a quiet volume. Conservation holds. There is no reservoir to tap.

The trail is already being dumped into that layer. Using the layer well is the assist.

- **Entrainment, in air.** The skirt folds the core into trail-plus-air the trail has already met. That air is the circulatory layer. Collapse is faster because the layer takes the structure and disorders it. Cabin or habitat air is still not the working fluid. The layer is not a new injector.
- **Dilution, in vacuum.** The vacuum layer does not cool by contact. It takes radiation and lets an expanded remainder spread. Forcing a broader radiating area cooperates with that long-path dilution. It does not fight it.
- **A reading.** Downstream of the skirt, the layer holds the remainder. At the published standoff, a tight core still in the layer is a failed collapse. A wide, faint remainder is the harm line doing its job. The layer is an instrument. It is not a harvest.
- **Do not re-cohere.** A rare coherent template is a warning, not a tool. A mixer ring that holds a hot structure together is the failure this file already refuses. The layer’s default is decoherence. The skirt helps that. It does not interrupt it.

What the layer cannot do: supply the energy to run the skirt, replace $\Lambda$, $\alpha$, $\theta$, and $\tau$, or be cited as a reason the trail goes away. A remainder in the layer is still a residue.

## Tributary

A thermoelectric skin on the skirt may recover low-grade heat.

$$
P_{\mathrm{trib}} = \eta \, \dot{Q}_{\mathrm{skirt}}
$$

$\eta$ is the skin efficiency the campaign measures. $\dot{Q}_{\mathrm{skirt}}$ is heat into the skin, not the source power.

$P_{\mathrm{trib}}$ is reported. It is not a pass line. A skirt that recovers well and does not collapse has failed. A skirt that collapses and recovers nothing may still pass.

The skin does not share a driver with a [Mag-Path](MAG_PATH.md) lot, an [IRIS](IRIS_MIDS.md) coil, or an [RLC](REGENERATIVE_LATTICE_CORE.md) plate. Heat may join a cabin or a yard bus. It does not cross a mate face.

### Acoustic recovery

A second tributary, not the first article. Acoustic energy in the skirt and in the near trail may be taken off as a low-grade electrical return, the same posture as the queued acoustic-thermoelectric note and as Mag-Path’s path tone: a medium already moving, read locally, not a new engine.

- **Where.** In the skirt skin and in the mixer throats. Not in the core. Not as a cabin tone.
- **What it is.** A short-path harvester on vibration the collapse already makes. It does not inject sound to break the trail. The tuned form of this coupon is [Resonant Recovery](RESONANT_RECOVERY.md). A skirt paneled by measured band. It does not replace this card. A failed coupon is a quiet panel and a standing skirt.
- **What it is not.** Not a pass line. Not a replacement for $\Lambda$, $\alpha$, $\theta$, and $\tau$. Not a comms channel. Not a log of the burn.
- **Window.** Published, and kept off Mag-Path’s grip band and off its acoustic window. The skirt and a walk line are not in one ear.
- **Fail.** Power loss on the harvester does nothing to the source. A dead harvester is a quiet skirt. The collapse still stands or falls on the four ratios.
- **Mass.** Counted. A harvester that weighs more than its campaign return, over a published duty, is refused. Recovery is not a reason to carry a dead mass.

Acoustic recovery may share the M0 bench. It does not replace the collapse card. M0-A, below, is the coupon. It is not required for a pass.

## Falsification

The theory is wrong, for a given source, if any of these hold on a published campaign.

1. $\Lambda \ge 1$ with the skirt on. The core did not shorten.
2. $\alpha \le 1$. The radiating area did not grow.
3. $\theta \ge 1$. The peak did not fall.
4. $\tau_{\mathrm{on}} \ge \tau_{\mathrm{off}}$. The core did not die sooner.
5. The source will not run with the skirt isolated.
6. A single mixer failure kills the burn.
7. The only way to pass is an injected mass the first article refused.
8. $S$ is lowered by hiding the trail rather than by collapse. A baffle between the trail and the instrument is not a pass.
9. $q(d)$ rises. The trailer or the dock is worse off.
10. The downstream layer still holds a tight core at $d$. The reading failed. Or the layer is cited as a fuel.

A failed campaign revises the article in public. It does not lower the pass line in private.

## Campaign M0

Bench, then a fired or vented source with the skirt removable. M0 replaces every orientation number in this file. M0 does not fly.

**M0, collapse card**

1. Publish $L$, $A$, $T_c$, and $\tau$ with the skirt off.
2. Publish the same four with the skirt on. $\Lambda < 1$, $\alpha > 1$, $\theta < 1$, $\tau$ down, or the pass fails.
3. At published $d$, publish $q$. Prefer $q_{\mathrm{on}} < q_{\mathrm{off}}$. A rise fails the harm line.
4. Kill one mixer. The source still runs. The ring still collapses, or $n_{\min}$ is revised in public.
5. Isolate the skirt. The source still runs. A source that will not run zeros the card.
6. Atmosphere and vacuum, or the instance the article claims. Do not average.
7. If a thermoelectric skin is fitted, publish $P_{\mathrm{trib}}$. It is not a pass line.
8. No instrument baffle. The proxy is of the trail, not of a hidden trail.
9. Publish $\phi$. A solid shroud is a failed geometry.
10. Downstream reading at $d$. A tight core still in the layer fails the reading. A wide remainder is the expected residue. The layer is not credited as a source.

**M0-A, acoustic coupon**

Optional. Not required for a collapse pass.

1. Publish the harvester window. It is off the Mag-Path grip band and off the path acoustic window.
2. Publish watts returned over a published duty, and the harvester mass.
3. A return that does not beat carrying the mass, on that duty, fails the coupon. It does not fail the collapse card.
4. Kill the harvester. The source still runs. $\Lambda$, $\alpha$, $\theta$, and $\tau$ do not depend on it.
5. No injected tone used as a mixer. Injection is not this coupon.

**What this file cannot finish**

$\phi$, $n_{\min}$, $\eta$, the band used for $S$, the clock choice for $\tau$, the standoff $d$, and the harvester window. Those wait on the bench. Writing them now would be a guess.
