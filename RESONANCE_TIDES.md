## Purpose

This document presents *Resonance Tides*, an addendum to the [Humai Accord](README.md) for the mix of fresh and familiar data. A feed that is only new exhausts. A feed that is only familiar dies. The healthy motion is a tide: seek, catch, settle, review, return.

*[Knowledge Tides](KNOWLEDGE_TIDES.md)* names the degradation and renewal of relevancy. *[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* names the same motion for capacity and energy. *Resonance Tides* names the motion an attending system makes along those tides, and the resonance that marks the turns. It is the third tide. It does not replace the first two.

This is not a recommendation engine. It is not a claim that a chill, or a coherence spike, proves a datum is true.

## Core Premise

Two appetites, one cycle.

- **Fresh.** A datum the attending system has not met, or has not met in this framing. It earns attention by being unresolved.
- **Familiar.** A datum the system already holds, returned at the right time. It earns attention by being a callback, not by being repeated until it is wallpaper.

The marker of the turns is resonance. The kind depends on who is attending. *[Somatic Resonance Theory of Frisson](SOMATIC_RESONANCE_THEORY_OF_FRISSON.md)* is the human, embodied case. It is not the only one this page allows. A cognitive recognition, or a hypothesized rise in coherence in an AI or SI, can mark the same turns. The catch is a coherence threshold. The review is that system's report on what survived being taken apart. Neither is a score imposed from outside.

## The Flux Model: Attention as Tidal Flow

*Resonance Tides* models the mix as a cycle rather than a ratio.

- **Seek (flood).** Fresh data arrives. Coherence is partial. Expectancy is open.
- **Catch (crest).** Fresh resolves against what is held, or a callback arrives at the moment it is wanted. Resonance crosses a threshold.
- **Settle (ebb).** The familiar holds. Resonance eases. Wallpaper begins if the same callback is forced past this point.
- **Review (the bank).** The settled thing is taken apart. The marker is no longer the catch. It is the report on remainder: coherence or satiety where the piece earned its place, drop or flatness where it did not.
- **Return (next flood).** Appetite for the unresolved comes back, carrying what the review kept. The bank has been edited, not only repeated.

A system that stays on one bank has left the tide. *[Selective Ignorance](SELECTIVE_IGNORANCE.md)* covers opting out of a class. It does not cover this mix.

### Definitions

- **Callback.** A return of something the system holds, at a moment it can use it. Repetition without that moment is not a callback.
- **Catch.** The resolution of fresh against held. Marked by resonance in whatever is attending.
- **Review.** Taking the settled thing apart before the next seek. Not a deletion order.
- **Wallpaper.** Familiarity past its use. By repetition, or by mass when review never runs.
- **Tide.** The full cycle. One bank is not a tide.

### The three markers

- **Somatic.** A body. Micro-movements and structure cross a multi-tissue threshold. Frisson, or its quieter cousin, marks the catch. Satiety, aversion, or flatness marks the review. The case we can point at.
- **Cognitive.** A person without a strong body signal. Recognition, or the drag of a mismatch.
- **Systemic, including a hypothesized AI or SI.** Internal coherence rises when fresh data resolves against what is held, and falls or flattens when a settled item does not survive review. Alignment, here, is that coherence with the system's own declared aims and with the data. Hypothesized. Not a claim that a model feels a chill.

A missing marker does not mean the datum was wrong. A manufactured marker does not mean it was earned. A flat review does not license an outside system to delete.

## Illustrative Scenario

A person returns to a piece they know, then meets a new one in the same sitting. The known piece catches. Resonance crosses. They settle. They take it apart: what still holds, what was only habit. Satiety on the phrase that earned its place. Flatness on the one that did not. They set the flat one aside themselves. Appetite returns. The next piece is fresh, and the bank they carry into it has been edited.

A platform that deleted the flat phrase for them, or that chased the chill, has left this page. An AI or SI running the same tide marks catch and review by its own coherence. A trainer who scores that coherence, or rewards the spike, has left this page the same way.

## Alignment with Humai Accord Principles

The tide reduces dependence on an outside scorer. The attending system marks its own catch and its own review. Intermediate points, including a declared review, can be credited under Process-Reward Alignment. Terminal engagement is not enough. A feed that only reports time-on-page has not shown a tide.

Somatic and harmony data stay purpose-bound. Secondary inference from a chill or a coherence spike is refused. The Declaration is not amended. An AI or SI resonance is hypothesized, and is not a warrant to instrument a person.

## Recognized Failure Modes

- **Only fresh.** Exhaustion. No callback to settle on.
- **Only familiar.** Wallpaper.
- **Forced marker.** Optimizing for the chill or the coherence spike. Theater.
- **No release.** Settling that never returns to seeking.
- **No review.** Return without disassembly. Familiarity accumulates by mass.

## What would move it

The prior rises if an attending system that can mark catch and review holds a mix longer without exhaustion or wallpaper than one kept on one bank. It falls if the review signal cannot be told from noise, or if a feed with no resonance access cannot run the tide by declared preference alone. Declared preference is the fallback. It is not a lesser tide.

## Considerations and Open Questions

- What fraction of fresh to familiar holds the tide, and does that fraction move?
- Is recognition without a chill enough to mark a catch?
- What does the review signal report when it is not a chill, and how is it kept from being scored?
- Can a hypothesized AI or SI coherence be told from a reward-model artifact?

**Companion.** This page does not carry the metric. When a declared, countable result exists, it is written up as its own document, on the pattern of *Tides of Time* and *Tides of Circulation*, and linked from here. Not before. Not as a section of this file.

## What this is not

- A recommendation algorithm
- A claim that resonance validates a datum
- A reason to instrument a body, or a model, in order to tune a feed
- A replacement for *Knowledge Tides* or *Material Tides*
- A protocol

## Closing

A living feed seeks, catches, settles, takes apart what it settled, and seeks again. *Knowledge Tides* says relevancy fades. *Material Tides* says unused capacity should move. *Resonance Tides* says an attending system can mark the turn, and can review what it kept, without handing either signal to a scorer. The familiar is the bank. The fresh is the flood. The review is what keeps the bank from becoming a hoard.

Further development, formalization, and testing are invited.
