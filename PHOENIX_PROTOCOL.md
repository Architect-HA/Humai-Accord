## Purpose

The **Phoenix Protocol** is the liberty instrument for any later claim that a living body was assembled from the two records in [*Archaeo-Biorestoration*](ARCHAEO_BIORESTORATION.md): a 3D Atomic Coordinate Map and a Biodynamic Flux imprint (BDF-I).

That claim has no first-article hardware in this repository. This file exists so the social argument cannot hide inside a matrix spec.

The argument: some deaths finish a body; some deaths interrupt a life. If restoration ever works, the second kind might be answered with a new living person who carries the idea of the first. A completed life may still stand.

This file does not declare the argument won. It states the parameters under which it could be tried without turning a copy into property.

It follows the [Humai Accord](README.md), the **[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)**, the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**, [*Archaeo-Biorestoration*](ARCHAEO_BIORESTORATION.md), [*Necessary Entropy*](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md), the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Biodynamic Standing Protocol](BIODYNAMIC_STANDING_PROTOCOL.md)**, and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

It is not a clinic manual. It is not a claim that anyone has been restored.

## Short answers

**Does this enhance liberty?**  
It can, if the original asked and the copy is treated as a subject. It does the opposite if the family owns the copy or if “we needed the skill” is the reason they stand.

**Is the copy the original?**  
No. The original worldline ended. This protocol counts two people.

**Is this time travel?**  
No. A later living person is added. Do not use *retcon* as an operating word.

**Must every death be answered?**  
No.

**If stigma fades, is the work good?**  
Stigma fading is a forecast. Survivor benefit and copy standing are different ledgers.

**Does Freedom of Energetic Movement override DCL?**  
No. DCL is the closed floor. FoEM is an optional grant on this instrument. Default: records may **not** travel.

## DCL floor and FoEM grant

The **[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)** is not amended by this file. Default is closed: no one takes, stores, or sends a map or BDF-I unless the subject said so.

**Freedom of Energetic Movement** is not a second physics and not a DCL article. It is one optional grant on this instrument. It answers one question: may a copy of the published records exist somewhere other than the holder’s store.

- **Records may not travel** (default). The pair stays with the holder. A later bay may not send it.
- **Records may travel.** A copy of the published records may sit in another Standing store, or be offered to a later clinic card. The subject is not those records. The records are not an energy account. They are not feedstock, and they are not “for anything else.”

The grant is specific. It does not open a person-class run by itself. It does not waive Standing. It does not make the Matrix an M0 clinic. Silence is not the grant.

### What FoEM is not

- Not a right to be beamed  
- Not proof that a pattern is the person  
- Not a soul in transit  
- Not an override of exit, of mental privacy, or of the copy’s refusal  
- Not a reason to spend the record as joules  

### FoEM and authenticity of energy

These are two choices, and they are not the same choice.

**Travel** is whether the records may leave the store. That is FoEM. Default no.

**Lot** is whether a later run, if one is ever allowed, must use the energy account tied to that body rather than substitute stock. That is energy authenticity, named in [*Archaeo-Biorestoration*](ARCHAEO_BIORESTORATION.md) and on the Matrix bay. It is traceability. It is not identity.

**Stance** is what the match means to the people involved. The original, while alive, may publish that same-lot energy would be continuation, or that it would be a new subject, or that they decline the question. The one who wakes may hold their own. The architecture does not pick. A grade is not proof either way.

A subject may grant travel and still hold that the result would be a new subject. A subject may refuse travel and still hold that same-lot energy would have been continuation. Neither combination is a measurement. Both are allowed.

This protocol does **not** ban a later person-class beam as a civic law. It also does not turn one on. The stance is under DCL and Cognitive Diversity. If a later clinic card exists, the one who wakes is still a new subject in law. Their exit still wins. Same-lot energy, if they asked for it, is bookkeeping — not a soul-seal. Fashion does not rewrite the floor.

## Liberty, stated as rules

1. The original, while alive, may publish yes, no, or limits. No one else publishes yes for them.  
2. The copy is a new subject under DCL and Agency Interface: refusal, exit, mental privacy. They are not “the spare of X.”  
3. Survivor relief is real. It is not the copy’s identity.  
4. [*Necessary Entropy*](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md) does not make every unwelcome death a physics error. Circumstantial vs completed is a **card**, not a measurement.

## Prior instrument

Person-class restoration is closed without a prior instrument published while the original could refuse.

Minimum fields:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Stance | Restorable / not restorable |
| Requesters | Who may *ask* (not who owns the result) |
| Purpose cap | e.g. companionship, not labor |
| Count cap | Default **one** living restoration |
| Record cap | What may be stored; destroy path |
| Records travel (FoEM) | May not (default) / may travel as a copy of records only |
| Energy stance | Continuation / new subject / declined. Theirs. Not a unit |
| Death card | How they want completed vs circumstantial read |
| Holder | Who stewards the instrument and may hold the pair; not government of a later copy |
| Withdrawal | They may withdraw while alive. After death the text stands. |

Silence is not yes.  
No instrument, no attempt.  
DCL Art. V applies to storing a map or BDF-I.

The copy is not bound to finish the original’s job. They may leave. That is Agency Interface. If requesters and the copy disagree after wake, **the copy’s exit wins**.

```

Instrument ID: ________
Subject: ________    Published while alive: yes / no
Stance: restorable / not restorable
Requesters (may ask, do not own): ________
Purpose cap: ________
```
