### *A Three-Layer Shear-Thickening Line That Locks Where It Is Loaded*

## Purpose

This document specifies a line and a net whose strength is a jammed fill caught by a reactive cage. The fill is a shear-thickening suspension. The cage is a helical lay that tightens under load and slacks when the load leaves. The skin is a looser fill for the hand.

It is an architecture and a campaign. It is not a stamped rope, not a free beam, and not a claim that a fluid holds a person by itself.

It follows the [Humai Accord](README.md), including *[Stormcrashers — Mars](STORMCRASHERS_MARS.md)*, [Gulpgates](GULPGATES.md), the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

## Scope and Limits

**In scope**

- A three-layer line: dense contained core, helical cage, looser contained skin
- A net of the same line
- A lay that tightens under load and relaxes after, a tidal lay rather than a permanent twist-lock
- An M0 that replaces the orientation numbers

**Out of scope**

- A solid tensile filament as the core
- A light beam that grows or carries the line
- A claim that the suspension alone holds a fall
- Water-cornstarch in vacuum or on Mars
- A permanent set. A line that stays locked after the load leaves is a failed line

## The Three Layers

**Core.** A contained column of dense shear-thickening suspension. High particle fraction, so a grab or a shock jams it. A thin impermeable liner keeps it in. The liner is containment, not strength. Soft at rest, so the line coils.

**Cage.** Helical strands around the liner, in a loose lay while the core is fluid. Not a filament down the middle. When the core jams and holds its section, the lay goes into tension, binds laterally, and carries the load around the jammed length. Unloaded, the lay slacks. This is the reactive stabilizer.

**Skin.** A looser suspension in its own thin liner, outside the cage. Lower particle fraction. It is the handhold and the abrasion face. It is not the strength. A torn skin leaves a caged core. The skin liner may carry a metered low-volatility film, the same idea as a [Separation Lock](SEPARATION_LOCK.md) wall film. The grit cuts the film, not the liner. Water as that film is refused off Earth. A dry skin is still a skin.

**Edge.** A rappel over an overhang cuts a sheath. The skin is the face that is meant to wear. A sacrificial film on the skin liner, and a published edge coupon in M0, are the article’s answer. The cage stays behind the cut. A line whose cage is the wear face has failed.

**Soak.** A fast lower and a hard stop put heat and a peak into the line. The skin is the energy soak. A published temperature rise on a fast lower is an M0 line. If the liner cooks, the card fails. The line is not a descender. A descender still does the lowering. The line’s job is to not be the thing that breaks.

**Length.** More line is more of the same line, paid out or on a reel. A telescoping chemistry is not an article.

**Glow, optional.** The skin fill may carry a two-part chemiluminescent mix, kept apart until a published shear or a break-seal. It lights the line in the hand for a situational pitch. It is a consumable. It is not a standing lamp, not a glowstick replacement as a product claim, and not required. A dark line is still a line. The mix stays in the skin. It does not enter the core. A mix that changes the skin fraction out of band, or that poisons the jam, is refused.

A grab shears the skin, then the core. The core jams. The lay catches that section. Let go, the core unjams, the lay slacks, the line is a line again.

## The Lay

Twist is how the cage distributes load sideways and tightens only where it is asked.

The strands are a helical braid, two directions, so a loaded section binds instead of unwinding. Pitch is loose at rest. Under jam the core stops necking, the helix angle steepens, and neighboring strands close on the liner. Load shares around the section and along a short length, not into one strand.

The lay is tidal. It tightens on the rise of a load and falls back when the load leaves. It does not ratchet. A lay that needs a tool to unspool has failed. It fails open, in the same posture as [Mag-Path](MAG_PATH.md): loss of jam is a soft line, not a trap. Alternating lay direction along the line, segment by segment, keeps a long pull from winding the whole rope into one spring. Segment length is $0.3\text{–}0.6\,\mathrm{m}$, orientation. Shorter and the line is a stack of knots. Longer and a pull winds one spring. M0 replaces it.

The core liner may carry a light helical bias, so a jam prefers to hold section rather than squirt. The bias is a guide. It is not a strength member.

### Optional Tick

A short acoustic pulse in the liner may be fitted for flexibility, not for strength. Vibration fluidizes a jammed pack. It does not jam one.

- **Release.** A tick after the hand leaves, so a section that is slow to unjam slacks on the published clock. The lay is still tidal without it.
- **Storage.** A tick in the coil, so a packed line does not set in the bag.
- **Not a hold.** No tone during a grab or a fall. A line that needs the tick in order to carry has failed. Off on power loss. A dead tick leaves a line that still jams and still slacks.

Duty-limited. Watt-hours per event published. A tick that does not beat doing nothing is refused. Not a cabin tone. Off any [Mag-Path](MAG_PATH.md) band if a path is near. No shared driver.

## The Net

The same line, counted as a mesh.

- Soft to deploy. Packed as a bundle.
- Locks at the node or the span that is loaded. Unloaded mesh stays compliant.
- One broken strand is a remainder, in the [Gulpgates](GULPGATES.md) sense. The mesh still holds. A single outlet for the load is refused.
- Nodes are independent. A shared skin across a node is permitted. A shared core is not.

## What Carries a Fall (What Soaks the Load Shock)

The cage, engaged by the jam. Not the fill.

If the core does not jam, the lay stays slack and the line is not a rope. That is a published failure, not a hidden one. A line has a life. A fill out of date is a failed fill. The liner still contains it. The person does not trust it.

Peak load is carried as tension in the lay, fed by the jammed core holding its section. The picture is a finger-trap around a swollen core. That picture is orientation. M0 replaces it with a measured jam pressure and a measured lay tension. The skin soaks the bite. It does not take the peak.

## Environments

Earth is the first article. Water is a permitted carrier there, if the liners hold it.

Vacuum and Mars are a materials change, not a new article. A water fill will boil, freeze, or dry. Both fills use a low-volatility carrier, the same lesson as the film in [Stormcrashers — Mars](STORMCRASHERS_MARS.md). The jamming is the particles. The brand name is not the article. A liner that leaks is a failed liner.

## Fills

The jam is the particles. The carrier is what lets them move until the grab, and what keeps them from boiling, freezing, or drying. Core and skin use the same family. They do not use the same fraction.

**Earth, first article.**

| Fill | Carrier | Particles | Where |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Starch | Water | Cornstarch, fraction in band | Core or skin. Bench and camp. Not off Earth |
| Silica | Water, or PEG 200–400 | Fumed silica, fraction in band | Core. A cleaner jam than starch. Skin if the fraction stays low |

Water is permitted only where the liners hold it and the swing stays above freezing. A frozen water core is a failed core.

**Off Earth.**

| Fill | Carrier | Particles | Note |
|------|---------|-----------|------|
| Silica–PEG | Polyethylene glycol, low molecular weight | Fumed silica | Low volatility. Survives a dry, low-pressure coupon. First off-Earth candidate |
| Silica–silicone | A published silicone oil | Fumed silica | Same job. Quote as its own line. Heavier |
| Silica–glycol | A published glycol | Fumed silica | Permitted if it stays mobile through the night swing |

Water as the carrier is refused in vacuum and on Mars. A humectant is permitted if it does not turn the fill into a paste that will not unjam.

**Core against skin.** Same carrier in both, so a leak does not make a third chemistry. Core at $0.50\text{–}0.58$. Skin at $0.35\text{–}0.45$. A skin as dense as the core is not a skin. A core too dense to coil is not a line.

**What is not a fill.** A solid filament. A magnetorheological slurry that needs a current to jam. A fill that stays locked after the hand leaves. Those are other articles.

M0 picks the Earth fill. A second card picks the off-Earth carrier. One pass does not license the other.

## Materials and Grades

The article is a dense fill, a tidal lay, and a looser skin. A strand alloy that keeps those three is permitted. It is not required to be exotic.

| Part | Grade 1 — first article | Grade 2 — permitted | Not the article |
|------|-------------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Core fill | Starch in water, or silica in water, fraction in band. Earth | Silica in PEG or silicone | A solid filament. Water off Earth |
| Skin fill | Same family, lower fraction | Same | A skin as dense as the core |
| Core liner | Thin impermeable polymer | Same, vacuum-rated | A load-rated braid used as the liner |
| Cage strands | High-strength polymer braid, loose lay | Metal strand if M0 says it slacks | A filament down the middle. A lay that ratchets |
| Tick, if any | Short pulse, release and storage only | — | A hold mode. A continuous drive |
| Skin liner | Thin, grippable, replaceable | Same | Armor that makes the line a cable |

Quote Grade 2 as its own line. Do not bury a vacuum carrier in the Earth prototype band.

## Assembly

1. Fill the core liner. Seal. A leak at this step is a failed line.
2. Braid the cage on a loose pitch, two directions, alternating by segment. Confirm it slacks on a soft core.
3. Fill the skin liner. Seal. Skin is not inside the cage.
4. Mark segment boundaries so a cut strand is countable.
5. Coil test. A line that will not coil is a failed lay, not a strong rope.

No step adds a core filament. A builder who adds one has built a different article.

## Join

Two lines meet at a coupler, not a knot.

A knot necks the liner and dumps the fill. It is refused. The coupler seals core liner to core liner, overlaps the cage strands onto a published ferrule, and leaves the skin butted or overlapped outside. The join jams and slacks like any other segment. A coupler that is the only load path, or that cannot slack, fails the card. One coupler is a remainder in a net. It is not a remainder in a single line. A line needs its joins.

## Campaign M0

Bench, then a drop. A pretty coil is not a pass. The numbers below are orientation predictions. A landing outside a band is published. It does not fail for missing the band. A missed jam, a lay that will not slack, or a peak in the skin fails the card. M0 replaces every figure.

**Predicted bands.**

| Quantity | Band | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| Core fraction | $0.50\text{–}0.58$ | Jams under a grab. Still coils |
| Skin fraction | $0.35\text{–}0.45$ | Grabbable. Not the strength |
| Rest pitch | $25^{\circ}\text{–}40^{\circ}$ from the axis | Loose. Pays out |
| Jam pitch | steeper by $10^{\circ}$ or more | Lay caught the section |
| Jam shear | by $10\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ | A grab, not a slow squeeze |
| Liner pressure rise | visible, order $10\text{–}100\,\mathrm{kPa}$ | Core held its section |
| Release | pitch back in the rest band within $2\,\mathrm{s}$ | Tidal. Not a ratchet |
| Drop mass | $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ over $1\,\mathrm{m}$ | A person, a short fall |
| Segment length | $0.3\text{–}0.6\,\mathrm{m}$ | Alternating lay. Not a stack of knots. Not one spring |
| Liner | $0.3\text{–}0.8\,\mathrm{mm}$, burst above $200\,\mathrm{kPa}$ | Contains the jam. Not the strength |

**Cases.**

| Case | Prediction | Fail |
|------|------------|------|
| Rest | Coils. Pitch in the rest band | Will not coil |
| Grab | Jams by $10\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. Pitch steepens. Gauge rises | No pressure rise |
| Release | Pitch returns within $2\,\mathrm{s}$. No set | Stays locked |
| Drop | Holds $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ over $1\,\mathrm{m}$. Peak in the lay | Peak in the skin, or a break |
| Core starved | Lay stays slack | A slack core that still holds is a hidden filament |
| One strand cut | Line or mesh still holds | Single strand was the only path |
| Thermal, non-water | Mobile through the published swing | Frozen core |

Under [Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md), a skipped case zeros the card for the claim that case was meant to support. A held drop does not wash a missing release test.

## Instrumentation

**Gauge.** One pressure tap on the core liner, mid-segment, range $0\text{–}500\,\mathrm{kPa}$, resolution $1\,\mathrm{kPa}$ or better. It reads rest, jam, and release. A line with no tap is not an M0 line. The tap is sealed. A leak at the tap is a failed liner.

**Drop frame.** A rigid frame, a published anchor, and a guided mass of $80\,\mathrm{kg}$ dropped $1\,\mathrm{m}$ onto the line. One load cell on the lay path, one on the skin path, both above the peak the drop can produce. The mass does not swing free. A pretty drop with no cells is not a pass.

Also:

- A pitch mark per segment, photographed or sensed at rest and at jam
- A date on the fill. A fill out of date is not a pass

A reading without a published method is not a result.

## Safety

**A missed jam is a fall.** The line is not a rope until the core jams and the lay catches. A climber treats an untested or out-of-date fill as no protection. The article does not claim otherwise.

**Containment.** A burst liner is a spill, not a held fall. A spill is a residue, in the sense of [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md). It is not nothing, and it is not a clean deck. Liners are the life of the fill. A liner past its published cycles is retired. A retired line may sit in a heartwood-analogue hold under the **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)**. It is not scored as simple under-use.

**Skin.** A torn skin leaves a caged core. It does not leave a safe handhold. Replace it.

**Net.** One strand is a remainder. Do not load a node down to one strand and call it a net.

**Utilization.** A line and a net. Not a restraint. Not a weapon. Under the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**.
