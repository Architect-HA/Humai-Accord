## Purpose

This document names the **Ghost Glass**: the wearable instrument family that tests the predictions in [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md). It is one **Multi-Emitter** embodiment — locate, couple, dissipate, and log residual signatures in the restorative regime.

First article is a **pair of gauntlets plus a harness**. Composite skin on the gauntlets is the namesake material: platinum-cure silicone (TPU alternate) with electrospun mullite nanofiber. That hybrid is **Ghost Glass**.

This file is an architecture and campaign document. It is not a stamped build packet. Fiber counts, coil turns, and firmware hashes are builder outputs after G0.

It follows the [Humai Accord](README.md), [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md), [*Somatic Resonance Theory of Frisson*](SOMATIC_RESONANCE_THEORY_OF_FRISSON.md), the **[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)**, the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Biodynamic Standing Protocol](BIODYNAMIC_STANDING_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Phoenix Protocol](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)**, [*Archaeo-Biorestoration*](ARCHAEO_BIORESTORATION.md), the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**, [*Empirical Demonstrations*](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md), and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

## Composite (the glass)

**Ghost Glass** here is not window glass.

| Layer | Planning |
|-------|----------|
| Matrix | **Platinum-cure silicone** default. Medical TPU is the named alternate |
| Crystal | Electrospun **mullite** nanofiber. Planning fiber diameter **$200\text{–}800\,\mathrm{nm}$**. Light veil (low basis weight; publish the roll card) |

## Emitters (four)

Hybrid **harmonic** (acoustic / electromechanical coupling into the substrate) plus low-fluence **photonic-pressure** assist (optical momentum at measurement fluence, not a cutter).

| Path | Default | Cap (G0-P) |
|------|---------|------------|
| Harmonic | **$100\,\mathrm{Hz}\text{–}5\,\mathrm{kHz}$** couple/detect band. Optional **$20\text{–}40\,\mathrm{kHz}$** short couple bursts | Mechanical drive stays below a published damage line for silicone-on-skin and for the named mineral coupon. No continuous high-amplitude lock |
| Photonic assist | **$850\,\mathrm{nm}$** class LED or VCSEL (NIR). Visible red is the named alternate | **$\leq 50\,\mathrm{mW}$** optical per palm. Combined optical **$\leq 150\,\mathrm{mW}$**. Sense-only detect uses the low end of that band |


| Seat | Job |
|------|-----|
| Left palm | Detect / couple |
| Left wrist | Dissipate (counter-phase / decoherence assist) |
| Right palm | Detect / couple |
| Right wrist | Dissipate |

Intensity grades **0–3** (0 sense-only, 3 still under G0-P). Phase-aware pulses. Spatial select: tight palm spot or wider diffuse field. Real-time feedback from the log suite. Power-loss: emitters off. No “keep driving.”

Photonic-pressure path stays in the restorative band. Directed-energy, heating-to-damage, and disintegration modes are excluded under **[Utilization Integrity](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**.

### Eye-safe use (hardware note)

$850\,\mathrm{nm}$ at $\leq 50\,\mathrm{mW}$ per palm is a measurement assist, not a pointer.

- Diffuse at the palm. No collimated beam. No sight glass that looks into the emitter.  
- Do not aim a palm at a face or an eye. Detect and Couple look at substrate or consented skin, not eyes.  
- Wrist seats are harmonic-first. If a wrist ever carries optical assist, it uses the same cap and the same no-aim-at-eye rule.  
- Power-loss and abort kill optical first.  
- Label the gauntlet: NIR assist, do not stare, class per the final LED/VCSEL card.  
- Final class (IEC 60825 or equivalent) is a shop stamp after the optic is picked. This file does not award Class 1 by sentence.

A later hardware packet inherits this note. It does not replace a lab safety card.

### Silicone-on-skin damage line (planning)

The gauntlet is worn. Harmonic drive and waste heat have to stay below a burn and a rash.

| Limit | Planning |
|-------|----------|
| Skin face temperature | **$\leq 41^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$** sustained. Abort if the inner face exceeds it |
| Couple duration | **$30\,\mathrm{s}$** then Detect or off (already on the mode card) |
| Harmonic feel | No numbness, no reddening, no pain. Any of those is a fail, not a “stronger couple” |
| Contact | Snug. Not a tourniquet. Fingers stay pink |
| Optical at the palm | Diffuse under the G0-P cap. No hot spot that marks silicone or skin |
| After a run | Visual check of skin and of the wear face. Marked silicone is scrap |

Publish a shop coupon test (silicone on a temperature strip, then on the operator’s own forearm at grade 1) before grade 2–3 on anyone else. This line is a wear limit. It is not a license to drive mineral or tissue to failure.

Stretchable thermoelectric band on the forearms: **Bi₂Te₃ / MXene** (or equivalent published RTB). Harvests skin–air gradient. Harness holds a small buffer cell for pulses.

Planning budgets:

| State | Draw |

| Idle (UI + log + RTB) | **$50\text{–}200\,\mathrm{mW}$** class |
| Pulse (emitters) | **$2\text{–}5\,\mathrm{W}$** for seconds, from the harness buffer |
| Bench tap | Named charger on the harness; not required in the field if the buffer is up |

Idle on the RTB. Pulse from the buffer. Buffer recharges from RTB and the bench tap. Afterheat through gauntlet skin and harness vents. Not an RLC cassette. Not $9\times 10^{16}\,\mathrm{J/kg}$.

## First Article Build Card

The first article is buildable from this card plus ordinary shop plant. Horizon names are not. A builder who wants Marrow-Link, Lazarus, or a Matrix seat waits for a file that does not exist.

**What a shop orders.**

- Two gauntlet blanks, left and right, platinum-cure silicone. Medical TPU only if that card is written
- Electrospun mullite veil, $200\text{–}800\,\mathrm{nm}$, basis weight $5\text{–}20\,\mathrm{g/m}^{2}$, lot ID and an XRD check on the first roll
- Four emitter modules: harmonic driver in the $100\,\mathrm{Hz}\text{–}5\,\mathrm{kHz}$ band, plus an $850\,\mathrm{nm}$ LED or VCSEL at $\leq 50\,\mathrm{mW}$ optical per palm, diffuse, not collimated
- Stretchable thermoelectric strip for the inner forearm, bismuth-telluride class or the named MXene alternate. Housekeeping only. Not a power plant
- Two small curved or flat forearm displays
- Harness: log board, temperature / humidity / pressure sensors, buffer cell, tether to both gauntlets. USB-C class or equivalent. Radio stays dark until abort is proven

**Assembly order.**

6. Prove the optical cap on a meter: $\leq 50\,\mathrm{mW}$ per palm, $\leq 150\,\mathrm{mW}$ combined.
7. Weigh. Log the roll lot. Then the first Detect, on a named mineral coupon, not on a person.

**First shot.** Detect, intensity 0, on a quartz or calcite coupon with a public method beside it if one is available. A quiet coupon is logged as quiet. Do not raise grade to force a name.
