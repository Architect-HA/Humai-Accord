## Purpose

This document focuses on the machine that [Dynamo Reignition](DYNAMO_REIGNITION.md) left out of its file. That page owns the valve, the belt, and the score.

This page owns the rig that leaves a **heat-spine** behind, and lets the rock seat it.

The article is not a tunnel. It is a counted spine. Short tensegrity segments, each a case and a conductive path, set under a pressure balance and clamped when that balance is drawn down. The close that killed every deep hole on the record is the assembly step.

The rig is not Day 1. A colony that is not yet stable does not cut. Bench and analog come first. A Mars spine comes after the colony can spare the power, the spoil, and a failed segment. Several months to several years after self-sufficiency is the earliest this page will talk about a belt. It will not talk about a dynamo.

This is a supporting technical instrument within the [Humai Accord](README.md). It is not a claim that a 40 km shaft has been drilled, that a vacuum holds a hole, or that a seated spine is a field.

## The article

The article is a segment. A path is a count of seated segments. A belt is a count of paths.

| Piece | What it does | What it is not |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Balance, while setting | Holds the hole open long enough to place a segment | A vacuum. An empty bore. |
| Spine segment | A short tensegrity case and the heat path, left behind | A transit liner. A road. |
| The close | Preloads the segment. The rock is the clamp. | A close with no segment in it. That is a plug. |
| Named fill | In the segment, or hooked at the handoff. CO₂ first. | An empty shaft |

The close is local. Balance is drawn down only at the segment just set. The column below the head stays balanced until the next segment is in place. A draw-down that dumps the hole is not a seat. It is a collapse. The segment is built to take the preload. A segment the rock crushes is a fail, not a seated article.

## What the rig has to do

Five jobs, in order. Skipping one makes a plug.

| Job | What it is |
|-----|------------|
| Cut | A bit, a thermal head, or a resonant driver. Spoil is produced and lifted. |
| Balance | A fluid column matched to the rock pressure at that depth |
| Set | A segment placed while the balance still holds |
| Close | Balance drawn down at that segment. The rock clamps it. |
| Fill | The named fill is in the segment, or the handoff names it |

Macro-plasticity is the reason for both the balance and the close. At depth the rock flows. An opened hole wants to shut. A vacuum makes that worse. The balance gives the rock nothing to flow toward, for long enough to set. The close, invited onto a segment, is the seat. **[Quake Columns](QUAKE_COLUMNS.md)** cases through and grouts the annulus. The rule transfers. The depth, and the use of the close, do not.

The rig is a surface plant and a string that leaves segments behind.

| Piece | What it does | Bound |
|-------|----------------|-------|
| Surface plant | Power, spoil, balance control, segment feed | Stays in the belt. A spill is a fail. |
| String | Carries the cut head and the next segment | Ends at the base of the local crust. Not a bore into the mantle. |

## Deep holes — what has actually been drilled

Nobody has a deep-crustal transit machine. The deep record is a set of scientific and geothermal holes, and they stopped because the rock closed.

| Hole | Depth | What it taught |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Kola SG-3 | $12{,}262\,\mathrm{m}$ | Deepest vertical hole. About $180\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ at the bottom. Rock went plastic. When the bit was pulled, the hole crept shut. |
| KTB, Germany | $9{,}101\,\mathrm{m}$ | A pilot hole first. About $265\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$. Temperature and stability stopped it short. |
| IDDP-2, Iceland | $4{,}659\,\mathrm{m}$ | About $427\,^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$. A deep hot hole is possible. It is not a seated spine. |

The lessons. A pilot before the deep one. Balance, or the hole closes on nothing. Temperature arrives sooner than the model. Plastic rock flows in when the bit is out. Kola had nothing in the hole for the rock to clamp. This page puts a segment there first. A $12\,\mathrm{km}$ record on Earth is not a $40\,\mathrm{km}$ path on Mars. P1 relearns the lessons. P3 does not skip them.

## Hole card

One segment, one card.

| Field | What it holds |
|-------|----------------|
| Site | Bench, analog, or belt. Named. |
| Depth | Reached, not intended |
| Cut | Bit, thermal, or resonant |
| Balance | Fluid and pressure. A vacuum fails the card. |
| Segment | In place before the close, or not an article |
| Close | Drawn down onto the segment, or not seated |
| Fill | Named, or not yet a handoff |
| Spoil | Mass out, and where it sits |

A segment without a card is a note. A close with no segment is a plug. A handoff is not an M1 pass.

## Scale

The rig scales by counting seated segments.

| Step | What changes | What stays |
|------|----------------|------------|
| P0, P1 | One segment, in a cell or an analog | Balance, set, close onto the segment. |
| P2 | A shallow Mars segment, after the colony is stable | Not the pierce depth. Not Day 1. |
| P3 | A path of seated segments toward the base of the local crust | Not one hero hole. Not a field. |
| A second path | A second count, its own cards | No borrowing of the first path’s seats. |

A belt is an array of paths. A planetary valve is not a scaled spine. It is the other page’s question, and it is allowed to fail.

## Campaigns

A claim that the rig works is a trajectory of seated segments, against a null. A deep number with no segment is not a result.

### P0 — Bench

Cut, balance, set, and close one segment in a cell. No Mars.

Pass: one seated segment, from two runs. Spoil out.  
Fail: a vacuum claimed as a lock. A close with no segment.

### P1 — Analog

The same order in rock, to a depth the analog can hold. Not 40 km.

Pass: a seated segment, balance and close logged.  
Fail: a collapse filed as a seat. A depth the analog did not reach.

### P2 — Shallow Mars

After the colony is stable. One shallow seated segment in the belt, or beside it.

Pass: a seated Mars segment, spoil inside the belt.  
Fail: a cut before the colony can spare a failed segment. Spoil on an instrument.

### P3 — The path

Only after P2. A count of seated segments toward the base of the local crust, on the order of $10\text{–}40\,\mathrm{km}$ where the northern lid is thin. Each segment set under balance and clamped by the close. Fill named. Hand off.

Pass: a path of seated segments to the named depth, fill on the card, ready for the reignition page’s M1.  
Fail: a close with no segment. A bore past the base of the crust. A dynamo claim.
