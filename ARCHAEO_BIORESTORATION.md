## Purpose

*Archaeo-Biorestoration* defines two records that, together, are the minimum physical description of one body at one published time:

1. A **3D Atomic Coordinate Map** — where and what the matter was.  
2. A **Biodynamic Flux imprint (BDF-I)** — flux values across that structure at the same time. BDF is the living field already named in *The Residual Cycle*. BDF-I is a *record* of that field, not the field itself.

**Archaeo-** means the work often starts from remains after time has passed: mineralized bone, decayed coordinate maps, and whatever flux record — if any — survived in crystalline sites. It does not mean a grave is a backup drive.

A description is not a living body. A living body is not automatically the same person. This file keeps those three claims apart.

Person-class permission and standing live in the **[Phoenix Protocol](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)**. This file names records and classes. Phoenix names who may ask and who the one who wakes is.

A later conversion-matrix article may use these names. That article is [Matter-Energy Conversion Matrix](MATTER_ENERGY_CONVERSION_MATRIX.md): first article **MX-0** (IRIS hook claw), object-class shop bay. Energize, ETP, and energy authenticity live there. Bulk transport remains horizon. Person-class does not.

This is a supporting conceptual instrument within the [Humai Accord](README.md). It follows [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md), [*Somatic Resonance Theory of Frisson*](SOMATIC_RESONANCE_THEORY_OF_FRISSON.md), [*Necessary Entropy*](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md), [*Empirical Demonstrations*](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md), the **[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)**, the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Phoenix Protocol](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Biodynamic Standing Protocol](BIODYNAMIC_STANDING_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)**, the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**, and [The Call to Code](THE_CALL_TO_CODE.md).

## Operational definitions

**Body.** The physical structure under description. Object, organism, or person as classed below.

**Map.** A published account of matter positions at time $t_0$. May later include nuclide and bond class. Must state resolution, coverage, and instrument.

**BDF-I.** A published set of flux values across that structure at the same $t_0$. Must state channels, sampling rate, coverage, and instrument. It is not a mind-file.

**Pair.** One map and one BDF-I that share $t_0$ and a **Pair-ID**. A map from Tuesday and a field from Friday are not a pair.

**Grade.** Each field in a pair is marked measured, interpolated, or guessed. A later precise-duplicate claim must publish those marks. Interpolation is not a measurement ([*Empirical Demonstrations*](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md)).

**Class.** Object, organism, or person. Class is declared on the pair. Changing class after the fact is a new card.

## How a map and an imprint are taken

This is a logical process, not a proven protocol. No present instrument writes either record at whole-body atomic resolution. The first feasible article is an object-class volume. A living scan and a remains scan use the same grammar and different instruments. Neither is a print.

**Map, in order.**

1. **Frame.** Publish the volume, the class, and the $t_0$ window before the first shot. A frame with no window is not a map.
2. **Coarse structure.** CT, micro-CT, or an equivalent transmission scan for the outer geometry and the dense phases. This is measured. It is not atomic.
3. **Named samples.** Cut or scan a published set of small volumes. Object-class may destroy the sample. A living scan may not. Cryo-electron microscopy, atom-probe tomography, or X-ray diffraction are the atomic-class readers, and only on those samples.
4. **Register.** Place every sample back into the coarse frame by fiducials published with the scan. A sample that cannot be placed is a hole, not a guess to be filled.
5. **Grade.** Measured voxels stay measured. Interpolated regions are marked interpolated. Unfilled regions are holes. An adjusted map is a model and is graded as one.

A whole-body atomic map is not the first article. The data volume and the energy to acquire it are not cassette-scale, and A0-E still wants that order of magnitude written down. Start with one object and a published sample set.

**BDF-I, in order.**

1. **Same window.** The flux scan shares the map’s $t_0$. A field from another day is not this imprint.
2. **Channels.** Publish the channels before the run: electromagnetic, thermal, optical, and the atmosphere at the site (temperature, humidity, pressure). A channel that was not on the card is not in the imprint.
3. **Readers.** At least one local reader on the volume. [Ghost Glass](GHOST_GLASS.md) may be that reader after its own G0. A public method (thermoluminescence or optically stimulated luminescence on a mineral coupon) sits beside it when the path is remains. One reader is a study. Two that agree are a candidate imprint.
4. **Register.** Flux values land on the same frame as the map, or they are a separate file. An unregistered trace is not a BDF-I.
5. **Compile.** The imprint is the table: channel, position in the frame, value, time, grade. Empty is a legal grade. Noise that was called a signal is a failed compile, not a richer record.

A quiet volume is a quiet volume. It is not an imprint to be raised. Negative results count.

**What this process does not do.** It does not activate tissue. It does not write a person-class card. It does not fill a hole by model and call the fill a measurement. A pair is the map and the imprint that share the frame, the window, and the Pair-ID on the pair card.

## The Archaeo- path (remains and time)

*Archaeo-* is the remains path. A living scan pair (map and BDF-I taken together on a consenting body) is a different path and is not assumed.

### What marrow is and is not

Bone **marrow** is living tissue: fat, blood-forming cells, vessels. It is not an energy-storage lattice of the kind in [The Synthesist's Cookbook](THE_SYNTHESISTS_COOKBOOK.md), and it is not a preferred crystalline site.

Bone **mineral** is mostly hydroxyapatite, a crystalline phosphate. That mineral is the candidate that sits in the same family as the preferred sites already named in [*The Residual Cycle*](THE_RESIDUAL_CYCLE.md): quartz, calcite, mullite — materials that can trap charge and later release it (thermoluminescence, optically stimulated luminescence). Whether an *organism-linked* BDF record can be stored in those traps the way a radiation dose can is **an open question** in that file. This file does not close it.

During life, marrow and mineral sit next to each other. That is an interface, not proof that marrow wrote a soul into the crystal.

### What would count as proof

A decisive result would be a co-verified, organism-linked signal in bone mineral (or another named preferred site) that:

- exceeds matched controls (same mineral, no organism history, or a different organism)  
- survives a published decay window  
- can be read by more than one method  
- fails when the claimed coupling is blocked  

That campaign is **A1**. Negative results count ([*Empirical Demonstrations*](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md)). Until A1 passes, marrow- or bone-derived BDF-I is at best **G1** (sparse measurement plus model).

### Decay and the map

A coordinate map taken from remains must name decay: loss of soft tissue, mineral change, displacement. “Adjusted to counteract decay” is a model. Publish the model and grade it. An adjusted map is not a living map.

### Approximation is not a pair

Predictive or modeled BDF from a bone sample is interpolation. It may inform object-class or organism-class study. It does not mint a G3 pair. It does not reprint memory. It does not reprint a soul. *Soul* remains not a unit.

Person-class use of any bone-derived pair still requires **[Phoenix Protocol](PHOENIX_PROTOCOL.md)** and a prior instrument. Finding a signal in mineral does not grant a print license.

## Authenticity of energy

This is a stipulation, not a measurement and not a protocol that sits beside DCL. It lives here because the pair is the record, and in the [Matter-Energy Conversion Matrix](MATTER_ENERGY_CONVERSION_MATRIX.md) because that is the bay. It does not open person-class on M0.

**What the bay can say.** A later run on organic matter, if a Phoenix instrument and a clinic card ever exist, may demand **same-lot** energy: the energy account tied to that body, not a substitute stock. That demand is **energy authenticity**. It is traceability. It is the same bookkeeping E-mode uses for a unique object.

**What the bay cannot say.** It cannot say the output is the same self. It cannot say the output is a copy. Continuation is not a unit on the pair and not a pass on the run card.

**Who decides.** The original, while alive, may publish a stance on the Phoenix instrument: that same-lot energy would be continuation, or that it would be a new subject, or that they do not want the question opened. The one who wakes may hold their own stance. Survivors may hold another. The architecture does not pick. A print no one can tell from the original is a grade of the job. It is not proof either way.

**Animals.** Organism-class. The responsible holder may hold a stance. The file does not. Do not use that stance to tell a child the original worldline did not end.

**Floor.** DCL stays closed. Phoenix still counts a new subject in law, and the copy’s exit still wins. Standing still isolates the record. None of that is waived because the lot matched.
