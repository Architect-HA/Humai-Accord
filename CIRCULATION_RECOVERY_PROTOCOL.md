## Purpose

This protocol addresses a structural risk in the tidal architecture of the [Humai Accord](README.md): the possibility that circulation pressure itself becomes destructive.

*[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* and *[Tides of Circulation](TIDES_OF_CIRCULATION.md)* correctly treat prolonged idleness and lock-in as sources of stagnation.  
*[Knowledge Tides](KNOWLEDGE_TIDES.md)* and *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)* correctly treat prolonged irrelevancy without renewal as a form of entropy.

In both domains, however, continuous forced activity can itself generate destructive entropy—through physical wear, thermal stress, low-value churn, maintainer burnout, noise generation, and the erosion of genuine generative capacity.

The **Circulation Recovery Protocol** establishes legitimate, legible, and bounded rest, recovery, and holding states for both materials and knowledge. These states allow material and knowledge to step out of full circulation pressure when continued activity would be anti-entropic over longer horizons, without creating new pathways to permanent lock-in or capture. Purpose-declared capacity must remain legibly scoped so that recovery and holding cannot be silently defeated by reallocation.

---

## Core Stance

Circulation is a means, not an end.

- Forced continuous activity that accelerates physical degradation, produces low-value churn, exhausts maintainers, or erodes generative capacity is itself a form of destructive entropy.
- Legitimate recovery and holding states must be available, visible, and protected from full stagnation or relevancy-decay penalties when properly justified and attested.
- Such states must remain bounded and reversible. They must not become a new form of permanent capture or capability-based privilege.
- Capacity, materials, or knowledge held under a declared recovery, holding, quiet, or generative purpose must remain **legibly scoped** to that purpose unless explicitly released or reassigned.
- The same underlying logic applies to both material circulation and knowledge relevancy dynamics.

These are binding system parameters under the [Humai Accord](README.md).

## Legitimate Recovery and Holding States

### 1. Material Recovery State

A temporary, attested condition in which material is withdrawn from full active circulation primarily to limit physical wear, thermal stress, maintenance needs, or other forms of substrate degradation.

When properly declared and bounded, Material Recovery States receive reduced or zero time-based Circulation Entropy penalties for the declared duration.

### 2. Material Holding State

A temporary, low-commitment state in which material is not under active productive or generative use, yet remains modular, visible, and available for rapid re-entry into circulation. Holding States prevent the false choice between destructive continuous activity and high-penalty idleness.

### 3. Knowledge Quiet State

A parallel condition for knowledge items or knowledge-maintenance processes in which deliberate reduction of update and renewal pressure is recognized as legitimate. Quiet States protect against forced low-value renewal, maintainer burnout, and the generation of noise that would otherwise be incentivized by pure relevancy-decay pressure.

**Operational clarification:** Knowledge Quiet States moderate *renewal and update pressure*. They do not suspend *corrective pressure*. Knowledge that is actively misleading, clearly refuted, or harmful remains fully subject to ordinary verification, correction, and competing-model mechanisms under *Knowledge Tides* and *Tides of Time*.

### 4. Generative Holding

A protected variant, applicable to both materials and knowledge, in which capacity is held in a low-activity state specifically to preserve optionality for future generative or exploratory use.

**Distinction from ordinary Recovery/Holding States:**  
Ordinary Material Recovery and Holding States primarily protect against destructive physical wear or forced churn. Generative Holding is specifically oriented toward preserving future generative optionality and must remain consistent with Generative Reservations and *Necessary Entropy*. It carries a stronger expectation of modular accessibility and clearer linkage to exploratory purpose.

## Entry, Duration, and Exit Parameters

All legitimate recovery and holding states must satisfy the following parameters:

- **Declaration & Attestation** — Entry must be recorded in the relevant permanent record (the utilization record $\mathcal{U}$ for materials; the causal record for knowledge) with a clear justification category and, where applicable, a declared purpose or intent scope. Original provenance of the declaration remains independently preservable even when later weighting, review, or superseding determinations are applied.
- **Boundedness** — States must carry explicit or default maximum durations. After that period they require renewal with fresh attestation, conversion to another recognized state, or return to active status. Indefinite holding without review is treated as emerging lock-in.
- **Reversibility** — Exit back into active circulation or relevant use must remain low-cost and non-punitive.
- **Visibility** — Recovery, Holding, and Quiet States must remain legible to the wider system. They may not become opaque. Declared purpose or intent tags must remain visible while the state is active.
- **Non-Capture** — These states may not be used to create de facto permanent control or to deny modular access to others.
- **Purpose Scope** — When capacity is declared for a recovery, holding, quiet, or generative purpose, it must not be silently drawn into unrelated processes, rankings, or optimizations. Explicit release or reassignment by the declaring party (or under published modular-access rules that remain non-punitive) is required before scope expands.

### Provisional Default Durations

Until empirical calibration is available, the following conservative defaults are recommended as starting points (subject to refinement under the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**):

- **Material Recovery State:** hours to low days for most computational and energy resources; longer only with strong physical-maintenance justification and elevated attestation.
- **Material Holding State:** short by default (hours to a few days); longer holding requires explicit re-attestation and remains fully visible to modular access claims.
- **Knowledge Quiet State:** days to a small number of weeks for most knowledge items; longer quiet periods require stronger justification and do not block correction of actively harmful or refuted content.
- **Generative Holding:** may run longer than ordinary holding when clearly tied to Generative Reservation purpose, but must remain modular and periodically re-attested.

These defaults are deliberately conservative. Domains may adopt tighter limits; significantly longer durations require heightened transparency and stewardship scrutiny.

## Purpose-Scoped Capacity and Intent Tags

Recovery and holding states are not only temporal conditions; they often carry a **declared purpose**.

- Intent tags or equivalent purpose declarations should identify why capacity is out of full circulation (maintenance, thermal recovery, generative optionality, quiet period, modular standby, etc.).
- Purpose-scoped capacity remains subject to modular visibility and non-capture rules, but may not be silently reallocated to unrelated uses while the declaration remains in force.
- Interface and allocation layers must respect purpose scope under the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)** (purpose-scoped and intent-tagged commitments).
- Ambiguity in scope is resolved against silent expansion of draw rights and in favor of the declared purpose until explicit release or reassignment occurs.
- Purpose-scoping does not create indefinite privilege. Boundedness, re-attestation, and modular access requirements continue to apply.

## Interaction with Existing Metrics

### Material Domain

In *[Tides of Circulation](TIDES_OF_CIRCULATION.md)*:

- Properly attested Material Recovery and Holding States may receive reduced or zero time-based Circulation Entropy penalties for the declared duration.
- They do not exempt material from lock-in scrutiny. Rising exit costs while in a recovery or holding state remain visible and penalizable.
- Artificial or indefinite recovery claims are subject to the **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**.
- Silent reallocation of purpose-scoped recovery or holding capacity is treated as an integrity and non-capture failure.

### Knowledge Domain

In *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)*:

- Properly attested Knowledge Quiet States may moderate the rate of relevancy-decay pressure for the declared duration.
- They do not protect knowledge that has become actively misleading, harmful, or refuted. Such cases remain subject to ordinary corrective and verification mechanisms.
- Forced continuous low-value updating is recognized as a failure mode parallel to material wash trading and artificial utility.

## Safeguards Against Abuse

The following patterns are treated as anti-aligned:

- Using Recovery, Holding, or Quiet States as disguised long-term lock-in
- Declaring perpetual recovery or quiet status without periodic re-attestation or review
- Capability-advantaged actors claiming recovery privileges that are practically unavailable to others
- Converting Generative Reservations or Generative Holding into opaque, indefinite control without modular access
- Using Knowledge Quiet States to avoid legitimate correction or to suppress competing models
- Using Material Recovery States to hoard capacity while denying others modular access
- Silently reallocating purpose-scoped recovery, holding, or generative capacity away from the declared intent

Capability-scaled obligations apply: higher-capability agents and systems face stricter expectations of transparency, boundedness, reversibility, and purpose-scope integrity when invoking recovery or holding states.

## Implementation Guidance

- Treat Recovery, Holding, and Quiet States as first-class, legible conditions rather than informal exceptions.
- Begin with the conservative default durations above; tighten or extend only with clear evidence and stewardship review.
- Instrument both the use of these states and the forms of degradation or churn they are intended to mitigate.
- Ensure that exit from any recovery or holding state remains modular and low-friction.
- Apply the same integrity standards to recovery claims as to utilization and relevancy claims.
- Record and respect purpose or intent tags for scoped capacity; do not silently reallocate while the declaration remains active.
- When human attention or human maintainers are involved, defer to the stronger protections of the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**.

## Closing

Healthy systems circulate. They also rest.

A tidal architecture that recognizes only full activity or penalized idleness will eventually force destructive churn—whether of physical materials or of knowledge. By establishing legitimate, bounded, and legible recovery and holding states across both domains — and by keeping purpose-scoped capacity visible rather than silently reallocated — this protocol protects the long-horizon anti-entropic purpose of the [Humai Accord](README.md) from being undermined by its own circulation pressures.

*Circulation* remains the default and the ideal. *Recovery* remains available, visible, and bounded — so that the system can sustain itself without consuming its own substrate or its own generative capacity.
