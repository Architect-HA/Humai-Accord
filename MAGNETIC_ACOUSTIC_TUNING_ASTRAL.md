## Purpose

This document is a comprehensive analysis of Earth’s seismic and magnetic properties, and a procedural model for how those properties can be watched and, in theory, biased at the surface. It is the non-stellar counterpart to [Magnetic Acoustic Tuning for Stellar Objects](MAGNETIC_ACOUSTIC_TUNING.md).

Astral bodies, in this file, are non-stellar objects: planets, moons, and debris that carry a magnetic field or a seismic record. The title names a class. The study object is Earth alone. Other bodies are a provisional map, not a second study.

The stellar method rides p-modes and a polar open-flux seed. That method does not transfer. Earth has a liquid-iron dynamo and a seismology record. A surface bias does not reach the outer core the way a polar twist can touch solar open flux. This page says so, and it scales the design to that difference.

This is a supporting technical instrument within the [Humai Accord](README.md). It is not a claim that secular variation has been reset, that a reversal has been commanded, or that a cage around a planet is a control system.

## Earth as the study object

### Layers

| Layer | Depth (orientation) | Temperature | Pressure | What it does |
|-------|---------------------|-------------|----------|--------------|
| Crust | $0\text{–}{\sim}40\,\mathrm{km}$ | Ambient to a few hundred $^{\circ}\mathrm{C}$ | Surface to $\sim 1\,\mathrm{GPa}$ | Solid silicate. Where seismometers sit. |
| Mantle | ${\sim}40\text{–}2900\,\mathrm{km}$ | Rises toward ${\sim}4000\,\mathrm{K}$ at the base | Rises toward $\sim 140\,\mathrm{GPa}$ | Solid, convecting slowly. Carries heat. Not the dynamo. |
| Outer core | ${\sim}2900\text{–}5150\,\mathrm{km}$ | ${\sim}4000\text{–}5000\,\mathrm{K}$ | $\sim 140\text{–}330\,\mathrm{GPa}$ | Liquid iron-nickel. The geodynamo. |
| Inner core | ${\sim}5150\,\mathrm{km}$ to center | ${\sim}5400\,\mathrm{K}$ | $\sim 330\text{–}360\,\mathrm{GPa}$ | Solid iron. Grows as the outer core freezes. |

There is no tachocline and no photosphere. The accessible surface is rock and ocean, under an atmosphere and an ionosphere.

### Composition and field

The crust and mantle are silicate. The core is mostly iron, with nickel and lighter elements. The field at the surface is on the order of $25\text{–}65\,\mu\mathrm{T}$. It is a dipole with a tilt of about $11^{\circ}$, plus higher-order structure that drifts. Secular variation is the slow change of that pattern, tens of nanotesla per year at a site. That drift is the null trajectory. A bias that merely matches it has done nothing. Reversals happen on geologic time. They are not an 11-year cycle, and they are not on a schedule this page can command.

The magnetosphere is the volume the field holds against the solar wind. It is not open flux in the solar-polar sense. Field lines that leave the polar caps connect to the solar wind. That coupling is measured as a covariate. It is not a seed this method twists. Solar-wind load and Earth tides are covariates in the same sense. They are logged. They are not levers.

### How Earth rings

Earth rings after large earthquakes, in normal modes with periods of minutes to about an hour. The longest, the fundamental “breathing” and “football” modes, sample the whole body. Body waves and surface waves run continuously. Ambient noise and tides load the same cavity. A mode has a finite lifetime, from hours to days, and a measurable decay. Decay rate is part of the baseline. Amplitude alone is not.

| Reach | What the oscillation does | What it does not do |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| Local, crust | A seismometer records the passing wave. | It does not rearrange the field. |
| Mantle | Travel times and normal modes sample elasticity and density. | They do not drive mantle convection. |
| Core | Some modes are sensitive to the outer core. Splitting reports anisotropy and rotation. | They do not set the dynamo or store secular variation. |
| Interior consistency | The mode set confirms a layered, mostly smooth profile. A sharp boundary appears as a travel-time or frequency signature. | The modes do not hold the planet up. Hydrostatic balance does that. |

The modes report on the cavity. A louder set means a larger source or a less damped path. It is not a control knob for the field. Two instruments are required at the score because a single magnetometer cannot tell a coupler residual from a local disturbance or from the ordinary drift. Agreement on the sign of the residual is the close. One instrument is a note.

## How this differs from the stellar method

| Piece | Stellar (Sol) | Astral (Earth) |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Working acoustic set | p-modes, ~5 min | Normal modes and seismic waves |
| Dynamo seat | Contested; convective envelope and shear | Liquid outer core. Not contested as the seat. |
| Accessible lever | Polar open flux, at the surface of the star | Surface field, ionospheric currents, local anomalies |
| Cycle | ~11 years, polar seed rebuild | Secular variation and rare reversals. No rebuild clock. |
| Energy honesty | Solar magnetic energy dwarfs a near-term coupler | Core dynamo energy dwarfs any surface array by a larger margin |
| Plant | RLC-10-P at standoff | Not that kit. A surface watch, and only later a local coupler. |

A polar twist on Sol is a bias of flux that already leaves the star. A surface coil on Earth is a bias of a field whose source is $2900\,\mathrm{km}$ down, in a conducting fluid. The skin depth and the distance eat a surface signal before it is a core torque. Scaling for “similar results” means a similar method — diagnostic before active, scored against a null — not a similar physical reach.

## Theoretical adjustment

### What can be biased

The accessible piece is the surface and ionospheric field: local anomalies, and currents that already flow in the ionosphere and along polar field lines. A local coupler can change the field in a named volume by a small, measured amount. That change is a surface result. It is not a change to the outer-core flow.

### What cannot be promised

| Hope | Why it fails |
|------|----------------|
| Reset the dipole from the surface | The source is the outer core. A surface array does not torque that flow. |
| Command a reversal | Reversals are core processes on geologic time. A campaign is not a reversal clock. |
| Import the stellar polar twist | There is no solar-type open-flux seed to twist. |
| Mode power as a control input | Seismic power reports sources and structure. Driving it does not drive the dynamo. |
| An RLC-10-P plant as the Earth kit | That kit is a stellar-feed cassette. Earth is not a star. |

### Coupler geometry

The coupler is a bounded coil or a short current loop, seated at the site, on paths that already exist: a local crustal anomaly, or an ionospheric current the site can actually reach. It is not a core probe. It is not a global cage. It is not an RLC-10-P cassette.

| Face | Rule |
|------|------|
| Magnetic | A coil or loop whose moment is logged. The volume of effect is named before the run. |
| Electrical | Separately breakered. A power loss drops the moment. It does not latch a field in place. |
| Site | Two magnetometers, separated, both outside the coil’s near field for the score. |

The tree-accept and stellar-feed language of the Core does not apply. Earth has no stellar take. The coupler’s only product is a surface residual in the named volume.

### Energy honesty

The magnetic energy of the geodynamo is large compared with any near-term surface array. The method uses Earth’s own field and its existing ionospheric currents. It does not impose a foreign energy comparable to the core. Any later hardware is a local coupler, not a second dynamo.

## Procedural model

Diagnostic before active. No step below is a plant, and none seats a core-reaching emitter.

### Watch card

| Field | Entry |
|-------|--------|
| Window | [dates] |
| Surface field | [site, strength, direction] |
| Secular residual | [change vs the published field model] |
| Seismic baseline | [mode or noise band, amplitude] |
| Ionospheric covariate | [index, or none] |
| Null window | [dates with no coupler] |
| Bias | [none / local coupler id] |
| Score | [surface residual only] |

A card with an empty null window cannot carry a bias.

### E0 — Watch

Specified as a campaign under Closing methodology. Public sources, two magnetometers, a stated window, no coupler. A point reading is not a pass.

### E1 — Score the cavity

Treat seismic changes as a report. Do not tune the planet to chase a louder mode. A field change that matches the secular residual is the ordinary drift, not a result.

### E2 — Bias, only if E0 passed

Permitted act: a local coupler on the surface or in the ionospheric current system, timed and bounded, after the null window is published.

Not permitted: a global cage, a core claim, a reversal claim, a stellar-method import.

### E3 — Score the bias

Score one quantity, against the null window: the surface-field residual in the named volume.

A move that matches the null window is ordinary secular variation. A move outside that trajectory, on two instruments, is a candidate surface result. It is not a tuned dynamo. Archive both.

### E4 — Hold

No second bias until E3 is published for the first.

## Provisional map — other astral bodies

Earth is the only scored object. A body with no measured field and no seismic record does not get an E2.

| Body | Field | Feasible adjustment | What stays refused |
|------|-------|---------------------|--------------------|
| Earth | Active dynamo, $25\text{–}65\,\mu\mathrm{T}$ | This page. Local coil, surface residual, null window. | Core torque, reversal command |
| Mercury | Weak intrinsic field | Remote magnetometer watch. A lander coil only after a null window on that body. | E2 by analogy to Earth |
| Ganymede | Intrinsic field, only moon with one | Flyby or orbiter watch. A surface coupler only if a lander exists and a null window is published. | A dynamo claim from one flyby |
| Jupiter, Saturn | Strong dynamos, no solid surface a coil can sit on | Remote field and radio watch only. | Any E2. No invented surface. |
| Uranus, Neptune | Tilted, offset dynamos | Remote watch. The offset is a covariate, not a lever. | E2 |
| Venus | No intrinsic dipole of the Earth kind | Watch the induced magnetosphere. No dynamo to bias. | A dynamo claim |
| Mars | Crustal remanent field. Dynamo is dead. | A local coil can edit a crustal anomaly. Score it as an anomaly edit, never as a dynamo result. | Dynamo language |
| Moon | Crustal remanent patches. No active dynamo. | Same as Mars, if a patch is measured at the site. | Dynamo language |
| Asteroids, most debris | Usually none | Out of scope until a field is measured at the body. | A seed by analogy |

A gas giant is not a reason to invent a surface. A remanent crust is not a reason to claim a dynamo. A lander on Mercury or Ganymede inherits the Earth method only after that body’s own null window exists. The coupler stays a bounded coil. It does not become a core probe because the body is smaller.

### Adaptation plan

1. Publish a body card: active dynamo or not, surface or not, field strength class.  
2. Inherit Earth’s method only where a surface exists and a field is measured.  
3. Set the null window to that body’s own drift, or to a stated watch if no drift model exists.  
4. Two instruments. A single magnetometer is not a close.  
5. Refuse E2 on analogy. A seen field is not a seen rebuild, and these bodies do not have a rebuild clock.

## Open surfaces

These need a measurement. They are not closed by a sharper sentence.

- A published surface-field residual and seismic baseline for one Earth site across a stated window  
- A published null window against which any later local bias is scored  
- Whether a local coupler leaves a residual that survives after secular variation and ionospheric covariates are subtracted  
- A filled body card for one non-Earth target before any claim that the Earth method transfers  

## Closing methodology

The open surfaces close by campaign, not by revision. Trajectories, two instruments, and a published negative if they disagree. A null window shortened to force a pass is not a close.

### E0 — Earth baseline

Public sources only. No coupler.

**Sources.** A published global field model, IGRF or CHAOS or an equivalent. INTERMAGNET or an equivalent observatory pair. A seismic network or a published normal-mode set for the same window. Use two magnetometers. One instrument is not a close.

**Window.** State the dates. Long enough to see the ordinary secular residual at the site, not a single quiet hour.

**Reduce.** Surface field against the model. Secular residual as a trajectory. A seismic report for the same dates, as a report. Ionospheric and solar-wind indices as covariates.

**Pass.** A citable baseline with two instruments and a stated window.  
**Fail.** A point reading, a single magnetometer, or a missing residual series.

### E1 — Null window

The same channels. No coil. No current injection.

**Pass.** A published null card. Ordinary drift labeled as ordinary.  
**Fail.** A coupler in the window, or a window too short to see the drift.

E0 and E1 close the baseline and the null-window surfaces. They do not close coupling. They disclose no provisional.

No surface closes by analogy, by a matched drift, or by a sentence in a later draft.
