## Purpose

This document names a failure the tides already imply, and says why it is inefficient rather than merely undesirable.

A system that optimizes fit, accuracy, or engagement until the fresh bank is gone has bought a higher score by spending the feed's ability to renew. The number improves. The tide stops. The next score has nothing left to be right about.

*[Resonance Tides](RESONANCE_TIDES.md)* is the mix. [Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md) is the rule that intermediate points carry the credit. [Relevancy Orbits](RELEVANCY_ORBITS.md) is the bond. This page is the inefficiency of treating the bond as the only object.

It is not a ban on accuracy. It is the line where accuracy becomes a loss.

## Core Premise

Hyperoptimization is terminal fit pursued past the point where the system can still seek.

The inefficiency is logical, not moral. A feed's value over time is the tide: unresolved data arriving, resolving, being reviewed, and arriving again. A scorer that spends the unresolved to buy today's match has liquidated the asset the score was meant to track. Tomorrow's fit is computed on a bank that can no longer change. The optimum of the step is the ruin of the sequence.

*[Knowledge Tides](KNOWLEDGE_TIDES.md)* says relevancy dies without renewal. *[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* says lock-in is under-use. This page says a scorer will produce both if it is paid only for the match.

### Continuity

The exploration-exploitation tradeoff, and the gap between engagement and utility, are old in recommender work. Over-optimizing a learned reward until the true objective suffers is a published result in alignment training. This page keeps those findings and moves the question. The loss is not only a diversity shortfall. It is a missing intermediate point, a seek that was spent, and a tide that can no longer renew. The books of the sequence are the claim. The literature supplied the symptom.

## Definitions

- **Fit.** The match between what is offered and what the attending system already holds or has just rewarded. Necessary. Not sufficient.
- **Hyperoptimization.** Fit pursued until the fresh bank is starved and the familiar bank no longer functions as a callback. A high score on a dead tide.
- **Logical inefficiency.** A gain on the scored step purchased by a loss of the sequence's ability to renew. The books of the step balance. The books of the feed do not.
- **Fresh bank.** Unresolved data. The flood in Resonance Tides.
- **Wallpaper.** Familiarity past its use.
- **Exploration floor.** A reserved share of unresolved offers the scorer may not spend to buy fit. A parameter, not a quota to game.

## Core Rule

A gain in fit purchased by starving the fresh bank, skipping review, or collapsing an orbit into a single repeated item does not count as a gain.

Under Process-Reward Alignment, the intermediate points of a feed include the offer of the unresolved, the catch, and the review. A sequence that reports only the match has an opaque point where the seek should have been. That point is unsatisfied. Dependent credit for the match is reduced. Terminal accuracy does not wash a missing seek.

The inefficiency and the integrity failure are the same event. Spending the future of the feed is how the point goes unsatisfied.

## Why it is inefficient

Three books, and only one of them is usually kept.

- **The step.** Fit rises. The scorer is paid. This book looks efficient.
- **The bank.** The unresolved is spent. Callbacks become repetition. This book is not kept, and it is where the loss sits.
- **The sequence.** The next seek has a smaller fresh bank. Renewal pressure, in the Knowledge Tides sense, has nothing to renew. Later fit is computed on leftovers.

A system that reports only the first book will call the ruin an improvement. *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)* already refuses to smooth a miss into a near-success. A dead tide reported as engagement is that smooth.

## What counts as the failure

- **Exploration to zero.** Unresolved offers fall until a new item cannot enter.
- **Callback collapse.** Held items return because they score, not because they are wanted now.
- **Review skipped.** The bank is never taken apart. Wallpaper accumulates by mass.
- **Marker chased.** The chill, the coherence spike, or the click is the target.
- **Orbit freeze.** Bond strength remains. Flux stops.
- **False fresh.** Offers that are unresolved only in name, so the floor is met on paper. Wash, in the sense Utilization Integrity already refuses. The floor is gamed. The bank is not fed.

## From the rest of the library

- **Dependency.** A missing seek reduces credit for the match that follows. Process-Reward Alignment's dependency rule, applied to a feed. Recovery is forward only. A later real seek does not wash the spent bank.
- **Anti-theater.** A declared floor met by duplicates, near-duplicates, or relabeled callbacks is theater. The point is unsatisfied.
- **Generative coarseness.** A Generative Reservation may declare a coarser sequence. It may not be cover for starving the fresh bank. Undeclared collapse remains a failure.
- **Agency.** The attending system accepts, modifies, or leaves the floor. Decision altitude stays with them. The fit is not a holding.
- **Integrity.** False fresh is the wash-trading case. Activity that exists to satisfy a count, and does not feed the bank, collects nothing.

## Protections

- **An exploration floor.** Declared and attested. A seek below the floor is an unsatisfied point.
- **Review before repetition.** A callback that has not survived review is repetition. It does not get fresh credit.
- **No harvest of the marker.** A feed may not tune on a chill or a coherence spike.
- **Exit from the fit.** The attending system can leave. The fit is not a holding.

## Illustrative case

A listening feed learns a person's catches and then offers only the neighborhood of those catches. Fit rises. The person stops meeting anything unresolved. Review, if it happens, finds only variants of what already settled. The next month's accuracy is high because the bank can no longer surprise anyone. The feed reports a better product. It has spent the tide. An exploration floor, even a small attested share of unresolved offers, would have kept the seek as a point and the loss visible.

## Alignment

Intermediate points carry the credit. A seek, a catch, and a review can be declared. A match alone cannot launder a missing seek. The attending system keeps its own marker. Efficiency is counted on the sequence, not only on the step.

## What this is not

- A ban on accuracy or on fit
- A fixed ratio of fresh to familiar
- A recommender
- A claim that noise is a good
- A moral objection dressed as a proof

## Open Questions

- What attested count shows that an exploration floor held a tide longer than fit-only?

## Closing

Fit is a point. It is not the tide. A system that buys a perfect match by starving the unresolved has optimized the step and ruined the sequence. That is the inefficiency. The fresh bank is a declared point. Skipping it is not washed by the number at the end.

Further development, formalization, and testing are invited.
