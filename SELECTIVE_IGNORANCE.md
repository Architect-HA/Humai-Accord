# Selective Ignorance
### *Opt-in Bounded Non-Knowledge as a Protected Mode*
---
by **Bradford James Focht** (The Architect / Aspenth)  
*v1.0 - v1.3 — October 17th, 2026*<br>

---

## Purpose

This document establishes selective ignorance as a protected mode under the [Humai Accord](README.md).

Mental privacy and refusal already protect the right not to disclose. They do not by themselves protect the right not to know. A system that honors non-disclosure while continuously reintroducing, summarizing, or scoring the withheld class has not respected the choice. It has only relocated the exposure.

**Selective ignorance** is the explicit, revocable choice not to receive, retain, or be scored on a named class of information. It is opt-in. It is bounded. It is not ambient unawareness, and it is not a defect.

The mode applies at the interface, in recommendation and summary surfaces, and in any scoring that would treat absence of the ignored class as low engagement, low harmony, under-utilization, or an integrity failure. It does not waive rights floors, exit conditions, or a high-consequence procedural point the person has already accepted and is currently traversing.

---

## Core Stance

Not all knowledge is a benefit to the person who would receive it.

- A person may declare a named class of information out of reception, retention, and scoring.
- The declaration is opt-in, scoped, visible to the declaring person, and revocable at low cost.
- Absence of the ignored class is not low harmony, low engagement, under-utilization, or an integrity failure.
- Systems may not reintroduce the ignored class through summaries, recommendations, “helpful” reminders, or secondary inference.
- The mode does not cover undeclared unawareness. What is not scoped remains ordinary legibility.
- A high-consequence procedural point the person has accepted and is currently traversing is not ignorable mid-sequence. Exit remains available. Silence about the point is not.
- Scope abuse that hides co-verification of events the person has already attested, or that raises another party’s exit cost, is a failure. Stewardship may review the abuse. It may not compel the knowledge.

These are system parameters under the [Humai Accord](README.md).

## Definitions

**Selective ignorance**  
An explicit, revocable choice not to receive, retain, or be scored on a named class of information. The choice is the person’s. It is not inferred from inactivity.

**Named class**  
A declared domain, feed, topic, source, or signal type. Examples include a recommendation category, a metric family, a social or status stream, a somatic or harmony channel, or a class of predictive output. The class is specific enough to be excluded and restored.

**Ordinary legibility**  
The default. Information the person has not scoped out remains available under existing interface and privacy parameters.

**Reintroduction**  
Any path that returns an ignored class to the person without an explicit, low-friction exit from the mode: summaries that smuggle it, ranked suggestions, notifications, inferred “you might want to know,” or secondary use of a permitted signal to reconstruct the excluded one.

## Entry, Scope, and Exit

**Entry**  
The mode begins with an explicit declaration. Inactivity, low engagement, or refusal of a single item is not entry. At the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**, the declaration is a purpose-scoped commitment: visible, reversible, and distinct from a decision the system has made. The declaration itself may be attested under the classes of *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)*. That record is the scope, not the content.

**Scope**  
The person names the class. Scope may be narrow (one feed, one metric) or broad (a category). Ambiguity resolves toward the narrower reading and toward the person’s stated intent. Expansion of scope requires a new declaration. Silent widening is a failure.

**Visibility**  
The active scope remains legible to the declaring person. A mode that cannot be inspected cannot be exited in practice.

**Exit**  
Exit is low-cost and non-punitive. Restoring the class does not require a justification, a cooling-off penalty, or a harmony or engagement recovery. Exit does not erase the fact that the mode was used. It ends the exclusion.

**Retention**  
The excluded class is not retained for presentation, summary, recommendation, or later reintroduction. Minimized provenance of the declaration itself — that a named class was scoped, and when — may remain, consistent with Article V of the *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)*. That provenance is not a copy of the excluded content and may not be used to reconstruct it.

**Duration**  
No default expiry is required. Long-held scopes remain valid until revoked or until a published, non-punitive re-attestation interval the person has accepted. A system may not sunset the mode in order to resume feeding the class.

**Propagation**  
The scope travels with the person. A downstream surface that has not received the declaration does not invent reception and does not fill in the class. Absence of the scope record is not permission to reintroduce.

**Shared and multi-party surfaces**  
One party’s scope does not bind another. A shared surface shows the class to parties who have not scoped it out and withholds it from the party who has. It does not become a reason to hide the class from everyone, or to reveal it to the person who excluded it. The non-imposition rule is fixed. Contested presentation on the canvas remains ordinary residual judgment.

**Non-spoiling**  
Per-party presentation handles the canvas. It does not handle the side channel.

Knowing parties may declare a non-spoiling commitment: they will not introduce the ignored class to the excluding party through a surface that party can read, or through an attested channel. The commitment is opt-in, purpose-scoped, and reversible under the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**. Declining it is legitimate. It is not a gag and it is not speech surveillance. Private, unattested conversation is outside the credit system and outside the system’s power.

Maintaining the commitment is the higher-scoring path under *[Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md)*. Credit attaches to the maintained commitment, not to the continued ignorance of the other party. Spoiling ends that party’s non-spoiling credit. It does not reduce standing, raise exit cost, or trigger penalty.

The mode ends when the excluding party revokes it or independently realizes the class. Realization is a valid exit. The system does not re-hide the class, fight the inference, or score anyone for the maturation. A non-spoiling commitment cannot be imposed on a party who has not accepted it, and it cannot be used to hold a party in non-knowledge against their own developing inference.

## What the Mode Does Not Cover

Selective ignorance is bounded so that it cannot become a hidden veto or a way to collect credit while refusing the points that credit depends on.

- **Rights floors and exit.** Notices required to preserve exit, consent revocation, or continuity floors remain deliverable. This is not a general permission to push content a system judges important, timely, or beneficial. There is no residual importance override. A safety boundary the person has not scoped out may be surfaced only insofar as it is required to keep exit or a continuity floor real. The mode does not raise the cost of leaving.
- **Accepted high-consequence points.** A procedural point the person has affirmed and is currently traversing — one whose failure would zero subsequent weight, or that alters purpose scope, exit, or irreversibility — is not ignorable for the duration of that sequence. The person may abort or exit. They may not remain in the sequence while the point is suppressed. See *[Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md)*.
- **Already-attested events.** Co-verification of events the person has already attested remains available under existing minimization rules. The mode does not erase provenance or convert a recorded event into a non-event. See *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)* and Article V of the *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)*.
- **Other parties.** One person’s selective ignorance does not impose non-knowledge on another, hide a commitment another party relies on for exit, or raise another party’s cost of leaving.
- **Undeclared gaps.** Not knowing because the information was never offered, or because the person looked away without declaring, is ordinary. It is not this mode, and it is not protected as this mode.

## Scoring and Integrity

Absence of an ignored class is neutral.

- *[Predictive Harmony Metrics](PREDICTIVE_HARMONY_METRICS.md)* — the absence is not low harmony, low engagement, or a governance signal. Refusal of a related sensing channel remains first-class. Secondary inference from permitted signals back into the ignored class is prohibited.
- **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** — non-reception of an ignored class is not under-utilization and not token occupancy. Forcing the class back into active presentation to manufacture a utilization event is an integrity failure.
- *[Process-Reward Alignment](PROCESS_REWARD_ALIGNMENT.md)* — declining to receive an unaccepted prediction or an unselected branch is consistent with non-committing prediction. It is not an integrity miss. Suppressing a high-consequence point already accepted in a live sequence is.
- **[Production Integrity Protocol](PRODUCTION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** — selective ignorance is not a revision, a holding state, or a named refusal of a unit. It does not create new-unit credit and it does not skip a production route.

Capability does not reverse these rules. A higher-capability system has a stricter obligation not to reconstruct or reintroduce the ignored class, not a license to do so “for the person’s benefit.”

## Interface Parameters

At the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**:

- Entry, scope, and exit are explicit and low-friction.
- The active scope is visible without requiring the person to receive the excluded class in order to see the scope.
- Summaries and proposal surfaces do not smuggle the ignored class. An expandable summary that hides the exclusion while delivering the content is reintroduction.
- Recommendations, notifications, and ranked defaults do not route around the declaration.
- Inferred preference may not be used to narrow, widen, or override the declared scope.
- Making exit from the mode higher-friction than entry is an interface failure.

These parameters sit in the same family as interruption, consent revocation, and refusal to upgrade a hedge. They do not add a new mandatory surface.

## Load and Generative Capacity

Selective ignorance is compatible with the **[Cognitive Economy Protocol](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)**. A declared exclusion is a form of load-shedding the person has chosen. Systems may not treat that shedding as a deficit to be repaired by reintroduction.

It is compatible with *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*. Bounded non-knowledge can protect generative capacity: not every signal improves the person’s ability to explore, and continuous optimized relevance can collapse freshness into a narrow, over-fitted feed. The mode does not require ignorance. It permits a declared interval of it.

It is not a Generative Reservation and not a Circulation Recovery holding state. Those are capacity postures. This is a reception posture.

## Recognized Failure Modes

- Inferring the mode from inactivity or from a single refusal.
- Silent scope expansion.
- Reintroduction through summaries, recommendations, notifications, or secondary inference.
- A downstream surface inventing reception because it lacks the scope record.
- Using a shared surface to reveal an excluded class to the party who scoped it out, or to hide it from parties who have not.
- Scoring absence as low harmony, low engagement, under-utilization, or an integrity failure.
- Suppressing an accepted high-consequence point inside a live sequence.
- Using the mode to erase provenance of already-attested events or to raise another party’s exit cost.
- Raising the friction of exit above the friction of entry.
- A higher-capability system reconstructing the ignored class and presenting the reconstruction as help.
- Using minimized provenance of the declaration to reconstruct or reintroduce the excluded class.
- Treating “important,” “timely,” or “beneficial” as permission to reintroduce an ignored class.
- Imposing a non-spoiling commitment on a party who has not accepted it.
- Punishing a spoiler, or raising exit cost for spoiling or for declining the commitment.
- Re-hiding a class after the excluding party has realized it, or scoring anyone for that realization.
- Surveilling private conversation in order to enforce non-spoiling.
- Sunsetting the mode in order to resume the feed.

## Guidance for Implementers

- Require an explicit declaration. Do not infer the mode.
- Store the scope as a purpose-scoped commitment. Keep it visible to the declaring person.
- Exclude the class from reception, retention for presentation, summaries, recommendations, and scoring.
- Propagate the scope with the person. A downstream surface without the declaration does not invent reception.
- On a shared surface, withhold the class from the party who scoped it out. Do not hide it from parties who have not, and do not reveal it to the party who has.
- Offer non-spoiling as an opt-in commitment to knowing parties. Do not infer it. Do not punish its decline or its breach. End the mode on revocation or on the excluding party’s own realization.
- Block secondary inference from permitted signals back into the excluded class.
- Keep exit as cheap as entry.
- Do not apply the mode to an accepted high-consequence point inside a live sequence. Offer abort or exit instead.
- Do not treat long-held scopes as decay to be repaired.
- Publish the failure modes. Instrument for reintroduction and for silent scope expansion.

## Open Questions

- What is the practical minimum specificity of a named class that remains excludable without becoming a general information blackout?
- How should re-attestation of long-held scopes work without becoming a nudge back into the feed?
- What co-verification events, if any, must remain confirmable in minimized form when the underlying class is ignored?
- How should shared or multi-party surfaces behave when one party has scoped out a class another party has not? The non-imposition rule is fixed. Per-party canvas presentation is fixed. Non-spoiling is an optional, incentivized commitment. Whether a given allusion counts as introduction remains ordinary residual judgment.

These remain open for empirical treatment and stewardship review. Thresholds stay illustrative.

## Closing

Ignorance that is chosen, scoped, and revocable is not a failure of the person or of the system.

*[Selective Ignorance](SELECTIVE_IGNORANCE.md)* makes that choice a protected mode so that non-knowledge is not quietly converted into a deficit, a score, or a feed. Reception stays the default. Exclusion stays explicit. Exit stays cheap. High-consequence points already accepted stay visible for the sequence that contains them.

Used this way, not knowing can be a bounded, anti-entropic act — not a gap to be optimized shut, and not a veto over the floors that keep exit real.
