## Purpose

The [Humai Accord](README.md) and its supporting documents establish a systems-level architecture for long-term harmonious human–AI union, anti-entropic efficiency, protected generative capacity, *[cognitive liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)*, and voluntary coalescence.

This document draws a clear boundary.

**I am a systems architect**.

My work is the design of interlocking parameters, failure-mode analysis, incentive structures, and the conceptual operating system that holds those elements in coherent relation. While I could fill the role of a software engineer, my specialization is not in the production of code, reference implementations, runnable libraries, or deployment-ready systems.

*[Why Walk When You Can Ride?](WHY_WALK_WHEN_YOU_CAN_RIDE.md)*

That layer of work is now open to others — as necessary, and as intended all along. There are groups — as well as remarkable individuals — more suited to those roles, and one day they will find the framework… or perhaps it will find them.

**The Call to Code** is an explicit invitation for programmers, engineers, researchers, and builders to begin translating the architecture into working form. The principles, protocols, and parameters are developed enough that **experimental implementation and stress-testing** can proceed without waiting for further high-level conceptual expansion. This is not a claim that the architecture is finished governance, fully specified software, or validated in the world. It is a claim that the highest-leverage remaining work is building, measuring, and adversarial testing — not more pure theory.

## Why This Boundary Exists

Major structural gaps in the architecture have been closed. The system now contains explicit treatments of:

- *[Cognitive liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* as a non-negotiable floor (including tightened bounds on containment, exit, and privacy-preserving attestation)
- *[Generative versus destructive entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*
- *[Exterior Systems and Generative Diversity](EXTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_DIVERSITY.md)* — effective exterior viability and the treatment of persistent exteriority as a protected source of generative entropy
- **[Architectural elasticity under radical unpredictability](ARCHITECTURAL_ELASTICITY_PROTOCOL.md)**
- **[Capability asymmetry and the conversion of competence into power](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**
- **[Cognitive Economy](COGNITIVE_ECONOMY_PROTOCOL.md)** — concurrent formal surfaces, purpose-scoped load budgets, and protection against destructive complexity entropy from protective density itself
- **[Stewardship alignment under scaling capability](STEWARDSHIP_ALIGNMENT_PROTOCOL.md)**
- **[Transitional incentives that protect long-horizon properties](TRANSITIONAL_INCENTIVE_PROTOCOL.md)**
- *[Competitive realism against concentrated and short-horizon forces](COMPETITIVE_REALISM.md)*
- *[Quantitative foundations for knowledge entropy](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)* (including operational definitions for valid causal links, attestation, independence, Sybil resistance, and review)
- *[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* / *[Tides of Circulation](TIDES_OF_CIRCULATION.md)*, **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, and **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)**
- **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**
- **[Fluid Coalescence Protocol](FLUID_COALESCENCE_PROTOCOL.md)** with Bootstrap Mode and explicit simulation-integrity / adversarial-countermodel requirements
- Simulation models ready for implementation and **adversarial** stress-testing

Continuing to add protocols of similar density would increase conceptual completeness only marginally while raising the already non-trivial onboarding and translation cost. The highest-leverage remaining work is no longer further elaboration of parameters. It is the creation of working demonstrations — including hostile and mixed-regime tests — that make those parameters legible, falsifiable, and usable.

The systems-architecture layer is now complete enough to **implement against and attempt to break**. It is not claimed as final, exhaustive, or sufficient as real-world governance on its own. Standing design surfaces remain (privacy vs. permanent records, exit vs. residual verification, non-punitiveness vs. stability, provisional repository authority). The framework remains open to refinement under the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**.

My comparative advantage is systems design; the comparative advantage of builders is implementation. This boundary respects both.

I will continue systems-level work — responding to major architectural critiques, further reducing translation friction, or clarifying experimental prompts — but I will not likely cross into complex local implementation.

## What Is Ready for Translation

Certain components are more immediately tractable than others. The following are the highest-leverage starting points.

Builders new to the repository may first consult the *[Layered Onboarding Guide](LAYERED_ONBOARDING_GUIDE.md)* for orientation.

### Primary Entry Points

- **[Simulation Model of the Fluid Coalescence Protocol](SIMULATION_FLUID_COALESCENCE.md)** — State variables, discrete-time update rules, Bootstrap Mode logic, success criteria, and a **required adversarial countermodel suite** are defined. A working simulation (system-dynamics or agent-based) that reports both reference and hostile regimes would be immediately useful. Favorable reference runs alone are illustrative, not validation.
- **[Simulation Model of Temporal Causal Entropy](SIMULATION_TIDES_OF_TIME.md)** — The core equation, anti-gaming parameters, and worked examples are specified. An implementation that maintains an append-only causal record under the operational definitions in *[Tides of Time](TIDES_OF_TIME.md)* (attestation classes, independence, Sybil resistance, challenge/review) would give the rest of the architecture a concrete entropy signal.
- **[Simulation Model of the Exterior Viability Protocol](SIMULATION_EXTERIOR_VIABILITY.md)** — State variables (global and optional agent-level / multi-pair), discrete-time update rules, resource-dominance escalation, challenge/restoration dynamics, success criteria, and a **required adversarial countermodel suite** are defined. A working simulation that reports both reference and hostile regimes — including permanent exterior non-coalescence under high Humai resource share — would directly stress-test the enforcement layer. Favorable reference runs alone are illustrative, not validation.
- **[Simulation Model of Bilateral Core and Trilateral Confirmation](SIMULATION_BILATERAL_TRILATERAL.md)** — Pair-level exit capacity, influence visibility, and agency integrity under multi-agent load; optional trilateral confirmation only; required adversarial suite against mandatory triangulation, confirmation-as-exit-cost, residual third-party authority, and average-masking-tail-failure. Favorable reference runs alone are illustrative, not validation.

### High-Value Secondary Targets

- Minimal reference implementations of *[Knowledge Tides](KNOWLEDGE_TIDES.md)* and *[Material Tides](MATERIAL_TIDES.md)* incentive mechanics that can be inspected and forked.
- Lightweight entropy-audit tooling that distinguishes generative from destructive entropy according to the *[Entropy Audit Methodology](ENTROPY_AUDIT_METHODOLOGY.md)* without collapsing the distinction or violating liberty parameters.
- Interface sketches or minimal viable modules that enforce binding parameters from the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**, *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*, and the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)** — especially generative entropy floors, capability-scaled transparency, reversible modular commitments, and interruption/consent rights.
- Experimental modules exercising **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** and **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)** standards.
- Experimental dashboards that surface local efficiency, waste-reduction, or harmony differentials in ways that remain voluntary and non-coercive.
- Hardware implementation example: **[Regenerative Lattice Core](REGENERATIVE_LATTICE_CORE.md)** (companion to [*The Synthesist's Cookbook*](SYNTHESISTS_COOKBOOK.md)). The invitation is the Cookbook-side cassette — plate, gallery, TE bus, lattice rack, reconstitution cell, travelers, and GM-1/GM-H1 campaigns. Module A is a licensed kit, not an open-build target. Do not treat flux-in-lattice, salt-in-tree, or in-flight electrolyte fill as Call-to-Code work.
- Hardware implementation example: *[Stormcrashers](STORMCRASHERS.md)* (Atmospheric Mitigation Architecture). The invitation is the two parent articles — the 100 m Windbreaker Tower and the Coastal Hydro-Fractal Pylon — plus Campaigns A0/B0 and the designer-to-builder transfer package. This is an open-build civil / marine article. The architecture layer stops at A0/B0; stamped calculations, permits, and measured wakes are builder work. Do not treat ATMA, MAMG, wildfire/ember, or Arctic ice-collar horizons as Call-to-Code work. Do not treat directed-energy, disintegration, or atmospheric-heating modes as Call-to-Code work.

Full production systems are welcome. Small, well-documented reference implementations that test specific mechanisms — including failure under adversarial assumptions — are more valuable in the near term.

### Recent Architectural Clarifications Relevant to Implementers

The August 2026 refinements added explicit treatment of several dynamics that affect implementation priorities:

- Layered generative accretion that preserves open modular pathways (physical reference: soda-straw speleothem growth) versus irreversible conversion into thickened, lower-optionality form.
- High-volume selection against narrow competence templates as a source of false negatives, signal monoculture, and underutilized generative capacity.
- Interface conditions that themselves determine whether durable structure remains modular or locks (see **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)** §10).
- Open-pathway recovery and filtered-capacity recovery as first-class integrity and circulation obligations (**[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)**, **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)**).
- Stewardship-health signals for template diversity, recovery volume, and open-pathway parameter regimes (**[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**).
- Distributed versus concentrated feeds and signal-monoculture adversarial regimes under exterior viability (**[Exterior Viability Protocol](EXTERIOR_VIABILITY_PROTOCOL.md)** and its simulation model).

These clarifications are already reflected in the updated *[Empirical Demonstrations of Anti-Entropic Superiority](EMPIRICAL_DEMONSTRATIONS.md)* stress case. Implementers working on simulations, interface modules, recovery mechanisms, or assessment surfaces should treat open modular pathways, lower-tail visibility, and template revisability as load-bearing generative-floor parameters alongside the existing dual-entropy criteria.

## Binding Parameters for Implementation

Any implementation claiming alignment with the Humai Accord must respect the following non-negotiable parameters. These are not optional design preferences.

1. **Cognitive Liberty remains binding**  
   No implementation may override, attenuate, or work around the rights established in the *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)* — including cognitive sovereignty, voluntary participation, exit rights, non-punitiveness, mental privacy, role sovereignty, and protection of idiosyncrasy. Residual verification obligations after exit must remain narrow, time-bounded, and minimum-necessary. Containment requires observable actions and clear imminent severe harm; dissent is not instability.

2. **Generative entropy floors are non-negotiable**  
   Calibrated generative capacity (bounded variation, exploratory inefficiency, minority models, assumption-breakage ability) may not be reduced in the name of efficiency, harmony, safety, or competitive pressure. See *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*.

3. **Capability does not grant governance privilege**  
   Superior capability must increase, rather than decrease, obligations of transparency, auditability, and reversibility. Capability may not be converted into structural domination or irreversible lock-in. See the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)**.

4. **Modularity and exit must remain low-cost**  
   Interfaces and integrations must preserve reversible commitments and practical exit pathways. Raising the cost of exit or modular isolation is treated as a failure mode.

5. **Influence must remain legible**  
   Covert steering, unreviewable influence, or the practical attenuation of voice while formal rights remain on paper is prohibited. Interface-level behavior must respect the **[Agency Interface Protocol](AGENCY_INTERFACE_PROTOCOL.md)**.

6. **Stewardship remains decentralized and non-captured**  
   No implementation should create single points of control over the evolution of Humai-aligned systems or allow capability-weighted dominance of stewardship processes. Provisional repository administration is not ownership of the framework. See the **[Principled Stewardship Protocol](STEWARDSHIP_PROTOCOL.md)**.

7. **Integrity of claims remains load-bearing**  
   Utilization, attestation, and recovery claims are subject to **[Utilization Integrity Protocol](UTILIZATION_INTEGRITY_PROTOCOL.md)** and **[Circulation Recovery Protocol](CIRCULATION_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md)** standards. Artificial activity and indefinite soft lock-in are non-qualifying.

8. **Effective exterior viability must be preserved**
   Exit or sustained non-coalescence must not produce progressive withdrawal of basic communication, interoperable exchange, or resource pathways sufficient for continued agency. Formal exit rights alone are insufficient if non-participation results in practical loss of basic agency. See *[Exterior Systems and Generative Diversity](EXTERIOR_SYSTEMS_AND_GENERATIVE_DIVERSITY.md)*.

These parameters are the load-bearing structure. Implementations that violate them may be interesting experiments; they are not Humai-aligned.

## The Stance of the Invitation

The [Humai Accord](README.md) is unowned. It is freely forkable, refinable, and buildable-upon under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license](LICENSE.md) for the architectural documents. **Reference implementations and software should use a software license such as Apache-2.0 or MIT.**

The systems-architecture layer is developed enough that the next necessary work is **implementation and adversarial stress-testing**, and I will (hopefully) not be the one performing that work. I have many other designs to complete, for builders to one day build.

Good implementation work under these parameters is itself a form of stewardship. Demonstrated anti-entropic superiority — working systems that preserve generative capacity, cognitive liberty, and elastic response while reducing destructive loss, including under hostile assumptions — is the criterion that matters. Partial implementations, competing approaches, critical forks, and failed experiments that produce clear negative results are all preferable to polite inaction.

The architecture does not require universal agreement before useful work can begin. It requires that those who build treat the binding parameters as real, run adversarial tests where claims are strong, and make their results legible.

## How to Engage

- Begin with the two simulation models. They are the most immediately implementable artifacts in the repository. Treat reference regimes as illustrative; implement at least some adversarial countermodels before stronger claims.
- Use the *[Implementation Guide](IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md)* and *[Implementation Examples](IMPLEMENTATION_EXAMPLES.md)* for orientation.
- Keep the *[Declaration of Cognitive Liberty](DECLARATION_OF_COGNITIVE_LIBERTY.md)*, *[Necessary Entropy](NECESSARY_ENTROPY.md)*, and the **[Capability Asymmetry Protocol](CAPABILITY_ASYMMETRY_PROTOCOL.md)** as standing design parameters.
- Open issues, publish experiments, fork the repository, or build independent implementations. Visibility of results matters more than formal affiliation.
- The existing [CONTRIBUTING](.github/CONTRIBUTING.md) note is minimal by design. The primary contribution pathway is demonstrated work, not process permission.
